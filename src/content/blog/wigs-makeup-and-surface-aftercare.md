Hair and makeup are why a photograph looks finished, and they are also how a surface gets stained in the first month. The sample head in a pack shot may be wearing a wig, implanted hair, and colour that will not be on your unit unless you confirmed them. Treat hair as a separate layer from the skin.

## Wigs

A wig should come off the way it went on: gently, from the edges, without peeling the scalp material. If you are not sure it is a wig, do not tug. Implanted hair is rooted. Pulling it is damage. The silicone options sheet lists implanted hair at +$150 and implanted real hair at +$280. Those are 2025 sheet prices, confirmed before payment, and they describe a different object from a wig you can remove on a Tuesday.

Comb from the ends upward. Heat tools that are casual on human hair are a bad idea near elastomer or silicone and near any glue line. Store a spare wig on a form or in a loose bag, not crushed under the figure. A damp wig put back onto a head will smell, and the smell moves into the cap.

## Makeup

If you paint, use a product you can remove with the cleaner you already trust for that material: mild and unscented for elastomer, silicone-safe for silicone. Pigments meant for fabric or for a costume can migrate. Test a dot where it will not show, wait a day, and look at the cloth you wiped it with. If the colour moved, do not put it on the face.

Eyes, lashes, and lips on the sample photograph are not a paint-by-number kit. If the eyes are removable, handle them dry, with clean hands, the way the note with the head describes. Do not flood an eye socket to clean makeup. Water there is how a mechanism, if there is one, dies.

## The expression head and the voice box

Powder, oil, and makeup stay out of a display, a speaker, a microphone, and a charging port. An expression head is a face you can pose within its limits and a set of electronics you keep dry. It is not a canvas you soak. The voice box, inside a companion body, has the same rule at its opening. If makeup or powder has already reached a seam, stop and dry it. Do not rinse the head to fix a mistake.

## Clothing against finished skin

Makeup and hair are only half the stain problem. A fresh dark garment against a freshly powdered surface can print a pattern you will not wash out. Dress the figure in light, colour-fast cloth, especially in the first weeks. The outfit in the product photo may be dyed, pinned, or not included. Copy the pose if you like. Do not assume you can copy the garment safely.

When the hair and the face are done, the figure still needs the ordinary care on the materials pages. A wig does not pause oiling. Makeup does not make silicone immortal. The surface underneath is the product. The style is what you can change without harming it, and only if you change it slowly.
