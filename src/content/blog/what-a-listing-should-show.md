A clear companion-figure or AI-robot listing identifies the item, the basis of its price, its documented capabilities and the availability checks still needed. Before paying, you should be able to connect those details to a written offer for the actual configuration. A category name, attractive image or warehouse badge cannot supply that complete record.

Read a listing in two passes. First establish what the page offers. Then check which parts need a current answer for your destination and requested options. This article is an audit of the page and its evidence. The broader [buyer's checklist](/blog/buyers-checklist-before-you-order) covers your handling, delivery and ownership preparation.

## Identify the product before judging the offer

Look for a model identifier alongside the height, material and descriptive name. The identifier helps connect the gallery, specification and later correspondence. Similar descriptive names can refer to different bodies, materials or electronic configurations.

For example, the 164 cm F-cup STPE companion and the 164 cm F-cup figure with expression head #1068 occupy separate listings. The former is a posed companion, while the latter adds the described expression-head system and has its own material and connectivity questions. A listing should keep those records distinct even when the photographs show a similar body.

| Page element | Useful evidence | Question if it is missing or vague |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Product identity | Identifier, height, material and head description | Which exact configuration is offered? |
| Dimensions | Named measurements, units and source notes | Which measurements can be confirmed? |
| Price | Currency, price type, date and option breakdown | What does this amount cover? |
| Availability | Warehouse list or available-to-order explanation | What will be checked before payment? |
| Images | Captions explaining samples and overlays | Does this image show an included part? |
| Electronics | Specific functions and operating limitations | What behavior is documented for this unit? |
| Purchase process | Written acceptance, payment and support terms | What happens after the request is sent? |

## Check the meaning of the price

A public number needs a description. It may be a current figure price, a previous list price or suggested retail from an older catalog. Those meanings affect how you compare it with another offer.

On AIB Robot, STPE companions show $2,199 alongside a previous $2,599 list price as of 15 November 2026. Silicone companions use suggested retail of $1,900 or $1,950 from 4 October 2025. The expression-head figure is listed at $3,099 as of 15 November 2026. Each public amount is reconfirmed before payment.

Check the selected material before comparing prices. Also record which options were added and whether freight is included. The optional voice box is $100, pending confirmation, while the silicone options sheet's $30 Sound item is a different line. A complete quotation should identify the chosen option clearly enough to prevent substitution.

A crossed-out amount needs an explanation of what it represents. Here it is a previous list price. It establishes no particular percentage saving against every seller or configuration. For your own comparison, use the same material, selected options and shipping assumptions in both offers.

## Look for measurements with an identifiable basis

A useful specification table names measurements and units. It also explains where a number came from or why a field remains unfilled. Height alone leaves open the weight, proportions and clothing dimensions that may affect your decision.

The 165 cm silicone companion uses 32 kg from an October 2025 sheet and acknowledges differing weights in other records. That note tells you which figure is being used and what needs checking for the actual unit. A conflicting record should prompt clarification rather than an average invented from several values.

The expression-head product has no full body chart or net weight in the notes used for its listing. Its page therefore leaves those fields open. A similar companion body's chart does not establish measurements for a differently configured product. Ask for the information you need before selecting clothing, support or delivery arrangements.

## Read warehouse wording as evidence with a date

The US and EU warehouse labels on this site refer to lists received in November 2026. They identify bodies that can be investigated as existing units. Current availability, head, skin and material are reconfirmed before payment.

Look for that explanation near the stock label or through a clearly linked shipping policy. A listing also needs a separate path for products available to order, including how their configuration and lead time will be confirmed. The [availability guide](/blog/in-stock-vs-made-to-order) explains the practical difference.

The expression-head figure remains available to order even though a similarly sized companion appears on warehouse lists. An existing body and a separately configured electronic head require their own confirmation. Product identity should survive every stock label applied to it.

## Match image captions to the offer

A product photograph should explain whether it shows an example pairing, a particular available unit or an illustrative collection image. That information tells you what the image can establish.

AIB Robot retains printed overlays on supplied photographs. Some overlays differ from the specification table. Read the table as the listed specification, then ask about any detail you intended to select from the image. The discrepancy should remain visible in your notes until it is resolved for the order.

Clothing, wigs, props and supports need inclusion confirmation when they matter to the purchase. A close portrait can show the photographed face while leaving the body and material uncertain. Our [photograph guide](/blog/reading-photographs-and-overlays) provides a more detailed reading method.

## Audit electronics through actual functions

For a product labeled AI, identify the component that provides interaction. An audio module, expression head and independently moving body establish different expectations. The page should state the functions documented for its particular hardware.

The expression-head notes describe a display, microphone, speakers, wireless interfaces and limited facial and neck movement. The listing also identifies unresolved US or EU home-network behavior. The body is posed by hand and cannot walk. Those limitations belong alongside the feature description.

Questions about languages, ongoing services, power and setup should receive current answers before the purchase depends on them. A quoted battery duration from supplied notes should be identified as such. AIB Robot has not independently timed the expression-head standby and interaction figures.

## Follow the page into written confirmation

A product page provides the basis for an inquiry. The accepted offer should then identify the buyer's selected figure, material, head and finish, optional components, destination and final cost. Any change made during correspondence should appear in the confirmed configuration.

At AIB Robot, checkout is an order request. Card payments are not taken on these pages, and an inquiry bag entry creates no reservation. The [terms](/terms) explain written acceptance and payment arrangements. Keep your card details out of ordinary correspondence.

Read shipping and returns before accepting. Check how freight is quoted, how the packing method is confirmed and what inspection process applies on arrival. A promise about remedy should connect to the [returns policy](/returns) and the particular accepted order.

## Save a record that another person could follow

Keep the product URL, dated description, selected image and relevant correspondence together. The FTC recommends retaining purchase records and seller promises. [FTC online shopping guidance](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/online-shopping)

A useful final check is to describe the offer without opening the gallery. You should be able to identify the figure, material, head, included options, price basis, current availability answer and support process. If the description still depends on a phrase such as the one in the picture, send a more specific question before accepting the order.
