A warehouse list records availability at a particular point in time. Before paying, obtain a current answer for the actual body, head, material and finish you would receive. AIB Robot uses US and EU lists received in November 2026, then reconfirms availability and the final price for the requested configuration.

This second check connects a catalog choice to a unit that can actually be offered. It also catches differences between a sample photograph and the current configuration. Use the regional lists to find candidates, then keep the availability answer with your written order record.

## Understand what a stock list establishes

A list can identify a body in a warehouse region without providing every detail needed for an individual order. It may have been prepared before your request and may use a photograph taken for another purpose.

Consider a body that appeared on a list at the start of the month. By the time you inquire, a unit could have sold or become committed to another buyer. An image could still show an example head while the remaining configuration has a different head or finish. These are reasons to ask about the actual offer.

| Information | What the dated list tells you | What needs a current answer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Body identifier | The body appeared in that regional record | Whether a unit can still be supplied |
| Head photograph | The photographed pairing or sample | Head actually offered with the unit |
| Material | Description present in the record | Material of the offered configuration |
| Region | US, EU or overlapping list availability | Origin of the unit selected for your order |
| Options | Any options named in the record | Which requested additions can be supplied now |
| Timing | A basis for an availability inquiry | Preparation, dispatch and transit for the destination |

The separate [warehouse or made-to-order guide](/blog/in-stock-vs-made-to-order) helps choose your purchase path. Here the focus is the evidence needed to turn a dated warehouse entry into a current configuration.

## Ask about the actual unit

Begin with the product page and body identifier. Give the warehouse region you are asking about and your receiving country. If a particular photographed head matters, identify the image and ask whether it is the head currently offered.

Request confirmation of material, skin or finish and the head configuration in the same answer. A reply that the body is available can leave those details unresolved. A buyer who wants a specific appearance needs to know whether the existing unit matches it before assessing the price.

A current photograph, where available, can help connect appearance to the reply. Ask what the image shows and whether it relates to the offered unit. The presence of a photograph alone does not establish a stock hold, a unit condition or the inclusion of every accessory in the frame.

## Keep availability separate from a reservation

AIB Robot's inquiry bag stores a request in your browser. Adding a figure records your preference, but it does not reserve a warehouse unit. The site confirms a specific configuration and price before written acceptance of an order.

That distinction matters while questions are still being answered. A unit discussed earlier may need another availability check if the conversation resumes later or the requested configuration changes. Ask what the current reply covers when you are ready to proceed.

Avoid assuming that a numerical quantity would solve this problem. A published count can become stale after another sale, and it does not identify which head or finish belongs to a unit. This site therefore publishes the warehouse-list status and confirms the actual configuration for the order.

## Recheck options after the unit is identified

A requested option can affect whether an existing unit suits you. A different head, implanted hair or an audio module may require additional availability and fitting confirmation. The original warehouse entry cannot establish that every change is possible.

Identify the exact option. The optional voice box is offered at $100, pending confirmation. The silicone options sheet dated 4 October 2025 also lists a separate Sound option at an additional $30. Ask which can be supplied with the unit under discussion, including any fitting arrangement.

If a requested change is unavailable, decide whether you would accept the current unit or prefer another arrangement. Have an accepted change recorded alongside the body and head. That avoids a situation in which the stock answer describes one figure and the option answer describes another.

## Treat similar bodies as separate records

The 164 cm F-cup STPE companion appears on the US and EU warehouse lists used by this site. Its public price is $2,199 as of 15 November 2026, alongside a previous $2,599 list price. Those facts concern that companion listing.

The 164 cm F-cup figure with expression head #1068 is a separate available-to-order product, listed at $3,099 as of 15 November 2026. It does not appear on those warehouse lists. An existing body with a similar description provides no current stock evidence for the expression-head configuration.

Start an expression-head inquiry from its [own product page](/ai-robots/wm-164-f-1068). Its body material and current connectivity guidance need separate confirmation. Its body is posed by hand and cannot walk. A reconfirmation should preserve those product facts while answering the supply question.

## Confirm price and shipping with the stock answer

The public price and the actual unit need to meet in the same final offer. Silicone companions use suggested retail from October 2025, while current STPE and expression-head public prices are dated November 2026. A current stock answer alone does not reconfirm every price or option from those records.

Ask for the final figure-and-option breakdown and the freight quote for your destination. Freight depends on the actual origin, destination and unit weight. A body appearing in two regions can lead to different configurations or shipping arrangements that need evaluation.

Keep net weight separate from the packed shipment information needed for delivery planning. Ask about any missing weight or dimensions that affect access to your home. Read the [shipping policy](/shipping) for destination charges, packing confirmation and accurate customs information.

## Build a short confirmation record

A practical stock record can fit in one message. Include the product identifier, actual head, material, finish, accepted options, origin, current price and shipping arrangement. Keep the date of the reply so that you know when the answer was given.

For example, if you ask about the 175 cm silicone companion, refer to its page and request the actual head on any available US unit. The catalog uses a 38 kg net weight from its October 2025 sheet and $1,950 suggested retail. Ask for the current offer and any unit-specific information before treating those reference figures as a final quotation.

Save the reply with the image or description it references. The FTC recommends keeping seller communications and purchase records. [FTC online shopping guidance](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/online-shopping)

## Review changes before the order proceeds

If the requested unit has gone, compare any proposed alternative from the beginning. Check body identity, material, head, measurements, options and price. A nearby height or similar portrait can still describe a materially different purchase.

You can ask whether the original configuration is available to order and what lead time currently applies. An estimate for a confirmed warehouse unit cannot be carried across to a configuration that still needs to be made. Ask for the arrangement specific to the new offer.

Before accepting, compare the final record with the requirements that led you to the warehouse page. Confirm that any deadline, handling constraint or essential appearance detail remains satisfied. The [terms](/terms) explain written acceptance, and the [returns policy](/returns) explains why checking the configuration before purchase matters for intimate hygienic goods.

A current answer should let you identify the figure you are comfortable receiving and the arrangement under which it will be supplied. Keep that record available for inspection on arrival so you can compare the delivered unit with the actual agreement.
