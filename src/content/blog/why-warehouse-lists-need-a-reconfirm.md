A warehouse list feels like inventory. It is a snapshot with a date on it. The lists behind this site were received in November 2026. They tell us which bodies in this catalog were on a US list, an EU list, or both. They do not tell a buyer, weeks later, that a particular head is still on a particular body in a particular building. That is why every warehouse page on this site says we reconfirm before payment, and why we never print a unit count.

## What moves

Units sell. Heads are swapped. A body can be reserved by someone else between the day the list was written and the day you send a request. A photograph taken for the list can show a head that was paired for the picture and then removed. If we took payment on the strength of the list alone, we would be selling a memory. We ask instead: is a unit still there, and what head, skin, and material are actually on it? If the answer is no, you hear no before you pay.

We also do not publish the count. A number of units is not a retail fact you need, and it goes stale the moment it is typed. “On the list” or “not on the list” is the distinction that changes your order. The count does not, except as a temptation to rush.

## What the list never included

The expression-head figure was not on those lists. It is available to order at $3,099. Seeing a 164 cm F-cup companion on both the US and EU lists does not smuggle the expression head into the warehouse. The companion is the $2,199 STPE body. Different page, different product, no walking in either case.

Figures that were never on the list are made to order. Their lead time is confirmed for the configuration, not borrowed from the warehouse’s transit time. A warehouse shipment, when the unit is still there, is usually faster. “Usually” is not a number of days. We give you the number when we have it for your address.

## How to use the pages

Use the US and EU pages to narrow the catalog to bodies we can ask about as existing units. Use the shop when you are willing to order a configuration. Read the price date either way. Then send a request and wait for the reconfirm. The inquiry bag is not a hold. If two people ask about the same body, the person we can actually confirm a unit for is the person who can pay. That is ordinary, and it is kinder than a cart that pretends to reserve a figure it has not seen.

A list is a good filter. It is a bad lock. The reconfirm is the lock. Pay after it, not before.
