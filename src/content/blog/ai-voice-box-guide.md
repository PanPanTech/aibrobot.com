“AI” is being used as a label for at least three different purchases, and mixing them up is how people overpay or buy the wrong object. This guide is about the modest one: a voice box offered at $100, pending confirmation at the time of the order.

AIB Robot lists that add-on beside adult companion figures and beside one expression-head figure. The voice box is not the expression head. It does not give a figure a walking gait, vision, or a face that moves. It is an audio module. It does not depend on connecting an expression head to a US or EU home Wi-Fi network.

## What you are adding

You are adding a spoken-audio function to a soft figure you will pose, dress, and clean. The $100 figure is the price we are offering, not a markup we invented, and it is reconfirmed before payment. We do not publish a library of voices, a battery life, or a script of phrases for this box, because we will not describe a specification we have not documented. If a current note is available when your order is confirmed, we will pass it on.

On a product page you can tick the voice box when you add a figure to the inquiry bag. You can also name it in a contact message or an order request. We then confirm the current price and whether it can be fitted to the body and head you want. Fitting matters. A hard module inside a soft figure can create a pressure point, a gap, or a place that tears if the body is folded. Ask whether a basic function check is part of the order, and ask for the operating notes that come with the module.

## What it is not

It is not the 164 cm F-cup expression head listed at $3,099. That product has a display, expression movement, and a network question we have already refused to promise away. Adding a $100 speaker to a companion body does not move that body into the expression-head price, and it does not entitle anyone to call the body a walking robot.

There is a second, smaller audio line on the silicone companions. A 4 October 2025 options sheet lists a “Sound” option at +$30. That is not this voice box. If you want audio on a silicone companion, say which one you mean. We confirm what is actually available before payment.

The voice box is also not a reason to treat a companion figure as electronics-first. The body is still TPE, STPE, or silicone. It still needs cleaning, powder or the right surface care, support when you lift it, and protection from heat. The module is a guest in that object. Water, oil, and powder should stay off its seams, port, and speaker.

## How to think about the price

Keep the lines separate on the page and in your own notes. A silicone companion at $1,900 or $1,950 suggested retail, or an STPE companion at $2,199 beside a previous list price of $2,599, is the figure. One hundred dollars, pending confirmation, is the voice box. $3,099 is the expression head on a 164 cm F-cup body. A seller who folds the word “AI” into a single unexplained total is asking you to guess which of those three you are buying. Ask for the items written out.

The public prices on this site are reconfirmed before payment. A warehouse list is not a live unit count. None of that changes because you added a speaker.

## A short checklist

Before you add the box, write down:

- that it is a body add-on, not an expression head
- the current price, and whether fitting is included
- which figure it is being paired with
- how it is powered and switched on
- what must be kept dry
- what happens if the module is dead on arrival

If those answers are vague, leave the box off the order and buy the figure you actually wanted. A companion body with a clear care routine is a complete purchase. The voice box is optional. The expression head is a different product, with different limits, and it still does not walk.
