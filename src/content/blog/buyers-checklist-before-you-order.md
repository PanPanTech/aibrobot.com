An order request is the right moment to be dull and specific. The inquiry bag on this site is not a payment and not a reserved unit. Use it only after the list below is something you could show another person. The same list is a fair way to read any other seller. We would rather you use it on us.

## The product is adult, and lawful where it is going

You must be 18 or older. The figure must be an adult companion or the adult expression head, 157 cm and above. We do not sell child-like or anime figures. You are responsible for the law at the destination. If we will not ship there, the answer is no. Do not ask for a relabelled carton. We will not misdeclare contents.

## You can name the figure without a mood

Write the height, the cup if one is listed, the material, and the page you are ordering from. “The blonde one in the ad” is not an order. The expression head is the 164 cm F-cup with head #1068, listed at $3,099, available to order, and it does not walk. A 164 cm F-cup STPE companion at $2,199 is a different product. If you want one, say which.

## The numbers are on the page

Price, with its date. Weight, if we have it. Measurements we have, and a silence where we do not. Warehouse list or made to order. Voice box at $100 only if you ticked it, and the $30 sound option on silicone companions only if you meant that instead. Options such as implanted hair have their own prices on the 2025 sheet and are confirmed before payment.

## The photograph has been read as a photograph

Sample head, not a fixed head, unless we confirmed one. Overlay, if visible, is not the title. Collection photography is not a figure. You are buying the table plus the written confirmation, not the lighting.

## Electronics are described with their limits

If the order includes the expression head, you have read that home Wi-Fi in the US or EU is not promised and is confirmed before dispatch. If it includes only a voice box, you know that is audio, not a moving face. You know water and powder stay off ports and speakers. You know a dead-on-arrival module is a defect, and a network that was never promised is a different conversation.

## Shipping and money are still ahead of you

Freight is not included in the figure price. It is confirmed with the destination and the weight. Duties, where they exist, are for the recipient unless we agree otherwise in writing. Payment is not taken on this website. You will not email a card number. A change of mind after a sound delivery is not a return. Damage and defects are. You know where that policy is written.

## You can move it

You have a plan for the weight, the stairs, and a stand. You have a cool place to keep it, out of sun and out of a hot car. You know the first week is washing, drying, and posing, not a movie.

If any line above is still a guess, send a question instead of an order request. The contact form is there for that. A checklist that stops you is working.
