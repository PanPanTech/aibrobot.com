Before requesting an adult companion figure, confirm the exact configuration, whether you can handle its weight, the dated price, current availability, freight and the process for an arrival defect. For an AI expression-head order, add the material, operating instructions and unresolved home-network requirements. AIB Robot's inquiry bag records a request. The purchase proceeds through written confirmation of the actual order.

Use this checklist to prepare that confirmation. It starts with the figure you want and finishes with the inspection you will perform when it arrives. Keep a short record as you go. A question that prevents an unsuitable order is part of a successful buying process.

## Record the decisions that affect the order

A single page of notes is enough if each entry is specific. Copy the product identifier and the source date for a price or measurement. Where a detail is missing, mark it for confirmation. An empty field should remain a question until someone supplies an answer.

| Check | Record before requesting the order | Resolve before payment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Product identity | Product page, height, body identifier and selected material | Exact body, head, finish and included items |
| Handling | Published net weight and your access route | Unit weight where missing or inconsistent |
| Electronics | Desired audio or expression function | Module, fitting, power, network and operating notes |
| Availability | US list, EU list, both, or available to order | Actual unit or current production arrangement |
| Cost | Dated public figure price and requested options | Final breakdown, freight and agreed additional charges |
| Arrival support | Current returns and shipping policies | Inspection process and contact route for the accepted order |

Photographs help you choose a look. The information in this table establishes what you are actually requesting. Read our [guide to product listings](/blog/what-a-listing-should-show) if the relationship between a photograph, a specification and a quotation is unclear.

## Check adult product scope and destination

This site is for adults aged 18 and older. The current catalog contains adult companion figures from 157 cm upward and one adult expression-head figure. Child-like and anime figures fall outside the site's product scope. Check the actual listing rather than assuming that an image elsewhere in the category describes an item available here.

The destination also matters. The product must be lawful where the package will be received, and AIB Robot declines prohibited destinations. The [compliance page](/compliance) and [shipping policy](/shipping) set out the site's position. A plain outer carton still requires accurate shipping and customs information.

If you are uncertain about import requirements, establish the destination's current official requirements before committing. State the receiving country when making an inquiry. A warehouse being located in your wider region does not answer every question about destination eligibility, customs formalities or delivery to your address.

## Identify the body, head and material

Write down the model identifier as well as the descriptive name. A photograph of a blonde figure is insufficient to identify its height, body, material and head combination. Hair, eye color and makeup may belong to the photographed sample. Your final record should name the configuration being supplied.

For example, the 164 cm F-cup STPE companion and the 164 cm F-cup expression-head figure occupy different product pages. The companion's public price is $2,199 as of 15 November 2026. The expression-head figure with head #1068 is listed at $3,099 on the same date and is available to order. Choosing between them requires a decision about interaction as well as appearance.

Material remains an explicit confirmation point for the expression-head order because the notes mention both silicone and STPE body descriptions. Avoid carrying the companion body's measurement chart across to that product. The expression-head page leaves unsupported body measurements and weight unfilled.

## Make a realistic handling plan

Find the listed net weight before planning where the figure will live. The 165 cm silicone companion, for example, uses 32 kg from its 4 October 2025 sheet, with a note that other records have differed. The actual unit weight is confirmed before dispatch. Its height alone cannot establish how manageable it will feel to lift or reposition.

Walk the route from the receiving point to the intended room. Consider stairs, narrow turns, the place where you will open the carton and a supported surface for inspection. Ask about packed dimensions and packed weight if you need those to plan access. Product net weight and the weight of a complete shipment answer different questions.

Decide whether you can carry and position the figure safely with the help available to you. Consider where it will be stored and supported between uses. If the handling plan is uncertain, ask about a more suitable configuration before requesting payment details. Read the [care information](/care) before buying a stand or copying a pose from a photograph.

## Read the price date and option lines

The current catalog uses several price bases. Silicone companions carry suggested retail of $1,900 or $1,950 from 4 October 2025. STPE figures show $2,199 alongside a previous $2,599 list price as of 15 November 2026. The expression-head figure is listed at $3,099 as of that November date. All are reconfirmed before payment.

Record the optional voice box separately at $100, pending confirmation. The silicone options sheet also lists a different Sound option at an additional $30. Identify the one you mean. Requested hair or other options need their own current confirmation, including whether they are available on an existing warehouse unit.

Ask for a breakdown that shows the figure, each accepted option, fitting if charged, freight and any other agreed amount. Keep the currency beside the total. This makes it possible to notice a configuration change or a repeated option before accepting the offer.

## Confirm the availability path

A warehouse label on this site means the body appeared on a US or EU list received in November 2026. It supplies a starting point for a stock inquiry. Before payment, the actual unit and its head, skin and material are reconfirmed.

For an available-to-order product, ask about the current arrangement for the requested configuration. Lead time is confirmed in writing. The site publishes no delivery-day range borrowed from a general estimate, and the expression-head figure does not appear on those warehouse lists.

If timing matters, give the actual deadline and ask what can be established. Distinguish preparation, dispatch and transit in the response. A statement that production can begin does not establish a delivery date. The [warehouse and made-to-order guide](/blog/in-stock-vs-made-to-order) explains how to choose between those paths.

## Check what the photographs establish

Read the gallery captions and the specification table together. Some photographs use an example head pairing. Some carry printed overlays that disagree with the height in the current listing. Those discrepancies need a written answer if they affect what you want to buy.

Ask which parts of the image are included. A wig, clothing, decorative accessory or support used for photography can alter the impression of the figure without establishing an order inclusion. If a detail is essential, identify it in your request rather than relying on the seller to infer it.

The [photograph guide](/blog/reading-photographs-and-overlays) provides a method for checking overlays, lighting and sample heads. Save a copy of the particular image you are asking about alongside the reply, so that the question remains understandable later.

## Confirm electronics through specific questions

For a voice-box request, ask about the exact module, fitting, controls, power arrangement and operating notes. The $100 audio option has no documented voice library or battery runtime on its page. A feature available on another electronic product provides a question to ask, not an established specification for this box.

For the expression head, identify the languages and interaction features you need. Ask about the supplied setup instructions and what current connectivity guidance applies to your destination. The listing does not promise conversation or facial-expression operation on US or EU home Wi-Fi. The body is manually posed and cannot walk.

Also ask how to keep electronic openings dry during cleaning and what information support needs if the module arrives faulty. Save the answers with the order. Our [AI expression-head guide](/blog/ai-bionic-and-expression-robots) gives the wider feature and configuration context.

## Settle shipping and arrival arrangements

A product price excludes a freight quotation. The cost is confirmed for the actual warehouse or origin, destination and unit weight. Duties, taxes and clearance formalities are the recipient's responsibility where applicable unless a different arrangement has been agreed in writing.

AIB Robot requests plain, unmarked outer packaging and confirms the packing method with the order. Carrier tracking and customs records may still describe a shipment. Read the policy carefully if delivery privacy matters to you, then ask about the specific packing arrangement available for your order.

Plan to receive and inspect a large carton. Prepare space for the packing, the figure and the supplied accessories before opening it. If delivery to an alternative location is important, ask whether the actual carrier and service can support that arrangement rather than assuming a general pickup option exists.

## Read defect support before accepting the order

The returns policy distinguishes a sound figure supplied as described from transit damage, missing contents and factory defects. A correctly supplied figure cannot be returned simply because you selected the wrong size or changed your mind. That makes the configuration and handling checks especially relevant.

For a defect, contact AIB Robot promptly with photographs of the carton, inner packing and issue. The remedy follows inspection and the actual problem. Possible outcomes include repair, replacement, a partial refund or another appropriate agreement. The accepted order establishes a clear inspection window.

Electronic components are included in that process. An unknown home-network behavior and a component that fails the configuration confirmed in writing require different evidence. Retain the relevant setup correspondence. Agree the return method, address and freight responsibility before sending anything back. Read the complete [returns policy](/returns) before purchase.

## Send a request someone can answer

A practical message identifies the selected product, material, requested head or finish, audio option and destination. State any condition that decides the purchase, such as a handling limit, a required language or a deadline. Request confirmation of the actual configuration and a complete price breakdown.

For example, you might request the 175 cm silicone companion, ask whether a US-listed unit remains available, and request its actual head, material and freight quote. If you want the voice box, name the $100 option and ask about fitting. This keeps appearance, stock and electronics in the same written inquiry.

Save the reply and check every requested change before accepting. The FTC advises keeping order details and seller correspondence when shopping online.
