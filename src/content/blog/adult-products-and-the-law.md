Before ordering an adult companion figure, establish that the exact product may lawfully be supplied to your delivery address, confirm the seller and written terms, and obtain an accurate shipping and customs plan. AIB Robot is for adults aged 18 and over. Its collection is adult-realistic, with a current minimum body height of 157 cm, and excludes child-like figures and cartoon or anime lines. These are the store's scope rules. Local legal questions require the relevant authority or a qualified adviser where you live.

The useful outcome of a legal check is a decision about a particular transaction. Record the product, destination, date and source of the answer. A general statement that adult goods are available online provides little help when a carrier or customs authority asks about your own carton.

## Start with an exact product description

Prepare a short description before asking about admissibility. Include the adult appearance, material, body dimensions and intended private use. For an electronic configuration, identify the expression head, voice module, supplied power equipment and any wireless functions. Send a representative dressed photograph or specification page where the authority requests one. An accurate description lets an adviser distinguish the physical goods from unrelated software or ordinary display equipment.

The current [AI expression-head figure](/ai-robots/wm-164-f-1068) has a manually posed body. It has limited powered head and facial functions. Overseas home-network operation remains unverified and needs confirmation for the supplied version. Describe those actual characteristics when seeking guidance. An advertised category name such as AI robot does little to establish the treatment of its materials, electronics or adult use.

Save the answer with the configuration it concerns. If you later change the head, body, destination or optional module, ask whether that change affects the assessment. Advice concerning a non-electronic body may leave questions about the new charger or radio-equipped head unanswered.

When reviewing a legal reference, record whether it is an enacted rule, an official explanation or a proposal still under consideration. Check the effective date and jurisdiction. A news headline about a bill cannot establish the current treatment of a shipment, and an old forum answer may concern a different product definition.

## Check the destination at more than one level

Importation, sale, possession and public display can raise separate questions. A national customs answer concerns entry at the border. A state, regional or municipal rule may concern possession or conduct after delivery. A shared home, rental agreement or workplace may also have practical restrictions that deserve attention before arranging delivery.

Use current official sources where possible. For a US import, begin with the applicable customs information and ask the responsible port or a customs broker about unresolved product classification. For an EU destination, consult the national customs and consumer authorities as well as the European Commission's cross-border guidance. Avoid copying another buyer's experience in a different country into your own order file.

## Adult styling and age are separate checks

Confirming age at the website entrance and again during an inquiry supports the store's adult-only scope. It cannot establish whether a product is permitted in every destination. Likewise, the minimum height in this collection describes what we carry. It is not a universal statutory threshold or a substitute for reviewing appearance and presentation.

AIB Robot declines inquiries for child-like figures, child styling and excluded cartoon lines. This applies to body selection, photographs, clothing requests and electronic configurations. Adding an expression mechanism leaves those scope rules in place. Buyers should review the confirmed unit as a whole, including the head and styling, rather than relying only on a body measurement.

For a private purchase, provide only the identity or age information reasonably needed for the accepted process. Ask how any requested document will be received and handled before sending it. Public review posts and social-media messages are poor places for identity documents, delivery addresses or order paperwork. The website's [privacy page](/privacy) explains the information its current inquiry process handles.

## Customs paperwork must match the transaction

Request a clear itemised invoice describing the goods, their value and any separately supplied accessories. Ask who prepares the declaration, who handles questions at clearance and which charges the delivery quote includes. A discreet outer carton can still travel with complete and accurate paperwork.

The European Commission explains that customs authorities can check descriptions and values, assess applicable import charges and inspect restricted goods. Its online-purchasing guidance also warns that a falsely low value may result in seizure, additional costs or a fine. The classification and payable amounts depend on the actual transaction and destination. [European Commission guidance on online imports](https://taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu/customs/customs-procedures-import-and-export/importation/buying-goods-online-coming-non-european-union-country_en)

AIB Robot declines inaccurate descriptions, understated values and routes intended to bypass a prohibition. A proposal to call an adult companion figure an unrelated object should be resolved before payment. Ask for the accurate description and a lawful route in writing. If the destination cannot be served lawfully, the request cannot proceed.

Keep the commercial invoice and any tax or clearance receipt together. If a carrier later requests payment, compare the shipment reference, the charge type and the party responsible under your written agreement. A shipping price and a statement about duties are separate line items that need separate answers.

## Identify the seller and applicable consumer rules

A warehouse location tells you where a unit may dispatch. It does not establish the seller's legal identity, governing terms or the consumer protections relevant to the order. Ask for the business name and a usable contact address in the contractual documents. Confirm who accepts payment and handles a complaint.

Your Europe explains that EU consumer rules can also cover a non-EU online trader specifically targeting EU consumers, with possible difficulties enforcing rights against a trader outside the EU. An EU warehouse label alone cannot answer the coverage question. [Your Europe guidance on shopping rights](https://europa.eu/youreurope/citizens/consumers/shopping/shopping-consumer-rights/index_en.htm)

Read the [terms](/terms), [shipping information](/shipping) and [returns policy](/returns) before accepting a quote. Ask about any unclear phrase while there is still time to choose another configuration or decline the transaction. Mandatory protections, where applicable, remain relevant even if a sales document uses broad language about final sales.

## Separate withdrawal from defects

An unwanted but correctly supplied product raises a different question from a product that arrives damaged, incomplete or inconsistent with the agreed description. Explain which situation applies when contacting the seller. Keep the confirmation showing the promised material, head, supplied electronics and any verified connection method.

Where EU distance-sales withdrawal rights apply, a cooling-off period and exceptions need assessment under those rules. The Commission's business guidance lists an exception for sealed goods unsuitable for return for hygiene reasons after opening. Whether an exception applies depends on its conditions. Calling an entire category hygienic does not settle an individual case. [Your Europe distance-selling guidance](https://europa.eu/youreurope/business/selling-in-eu/selling-goods-services/ecommerce-distance-selling/index_en.htm)

For a defect report, photograph the outer carton, protective packing and affected area, then describe what happened and which written specification appears unmet. Request instructions before repair or return shipment. This preserves useful evidence while the seller assesses the appropriate process. It also keeps an electronic fault distinguishable from a network capability that was never confirmed for your version.

## Build a transaction record before paying

A compact order folder makes later questions easier to resolve. Save the dated product page, agreed configuration, delivered-price breakdown, seller details, destination assessment and order acceptance. Add the current care instructions and the electronic setup guide where relevant. These records help explain what you ordered without exposing private correspondence publicly.

For example, consider a buyer who selects a listed companion body, then adds an expression head and requests delivery to a different country. The warehouse note, electrical requirements and destination assessment all need review. Keeping the old quote unchanged would hide three material changes. The buyer should request a revised confirmation identifying the final goods and shipping arrangement before authorising payment.

Use the same method when a sales conversation offers an alternative head because the photographed pairing is unavailable. Ask for the replacement's adult appearance, dimensions and relevant features, then compare it with the original requirements. Accepting a substitution is a purchasing decision that should appear in the written order.

## Use a specific question when seeking advice

A useful message states the destination, exact product and unresolved issue. For example, ask whether a described adult-realistic silicone figure with a separately specified electronic head can be imported for private use, and which documents are required. Include the relevant facts the authority requests and keep sensitive material in an appropriate private channel.

For contractual questions, ask the seller to identify the rule or condition it intends to apply and how that affects the order. If an official answer and the proposed sales arrangement appear inconsistent, obtain qualified local advice before proceeding.
