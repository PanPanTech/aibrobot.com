A European buyer has two different questions, and shops often answer the wrong one. The first is whether a body appears on a warehouse list inside the EU. The second is whether the destination allows the product and what the shipment will actually cost. This page can help with the first. Only you, and the written confirmation of an order, can settle the second.

## What the EU list is

The EU warehouse page on this site shows the figures from this catalog that appeared on an EU warehouse list we received in November 2026. The set is smaller than the US list. One body appears on both. We do not publish how many units are in the building. Before payment we ask whether a specific unit is still there, and which head, skin, and material are on it. If it is gone, the figure becomes an order with a lead time, or we tell you we cannot offer it. We do not invent a replacement and call it the same thing.

A list is also not a delivery promise. When a unit is still in the EU, shipping inside Europe is usually faster than waiting for a figure that still has to be made. How many days, for your address, is something we say when we know. We do not print a range we have not confirmed.

## What the list is not

It is not a statement about every EU country. Import rules, product rules, and privacy rules are not identical from one member state to another, and they are not something a product page can settle for you. You are responsible for knowing whether a companion figure may be imported and kept where you live. If the answer is no, we will not ship, and we will not relabel the carton to make the answer look like yes.

The expression-head figure is not on the EU list. It is available to order. Its connectivity on a European home network is not promised. We confirm current guidance before dispatch. Do not read “EU warehouse” on a companion body as “the robot is in Europe.”

## Price, tax, and the carton

The prices on the product pages are the figure prices in US dollars, not a delivered EU price. Freight is confirmed with the destination and the weight. Duties and taxes, where they apply, are for the recipient unless we have agreed otherwise in writing. A dollar price that looks tidy can become a different number after freight and tax. Ask for that number before you treat the order as affordable.

Packaging is requested as a plain outer carton, without product photography or a product name on the outside. We confirm the method with the order. We cannot control a carrier’s tracking description. We can control our paperwork, and the paperwork will describe the goods truthfully.

## Care and voltage are still your house

A figure that arrives in the EU is the same TPE, STPE, or silicone object. Heat in a top-floor flat, dye from clothing, and a stand that is too light are the same problems as anywhere else. If you added a voice box or you ordered the expression head, check the power notes that come with the unit before you assume a plug. We will pass on the charging guidance we actually have. We will not invent a European adapter story.

If you are comparing a body on the EU list with the same height available only to order, the useful difference is time and the head that is actually fitted, not a myth about the material changing because it crossed a border. Confirm the unit. Then decide.
