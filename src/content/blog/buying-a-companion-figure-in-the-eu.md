A body on an EU warehouse list is a starting point for that conversation. It leaves the fitted head, current stock, destination rules and electrical options to be checked before payment.

## What an EU warehouse listing establishes

The warehouse information here comes from lists received in November 2026. The EU selection is smaller than the US selection. The list does not publish live unit counts and does not reserve a figure while it sits in an inquiry bag. Before payment, we check whether the selected body is still available and what head, skin tone and material are actually fitted.

Ask for confirmation that the pictured or proposed unit corresponds to that description. A warehouse may carry a body with a different head from the example photograph. Decide whether that pairing meets your requirements before accepting it. If the listed unit has sold, ask for a new proposal and lead time rather than assuming that a replacement will arrive under the original conditions.

The [expression-head configuration](/ai-robots/wm-164-f-1068) is available to order. It is absent from the EU warehouse list used on this site. The conventional 164 cm body and the electronic configuration are separate listings with separate evidence about availability and specifications.

## Establish where the goods will actually begin their journey

Europe and the EU customs territory are not interchangeable labels. Give the full destination country and postcode, and ask for the dispatch country. A UK or Swiss address, for example, needs its own route assessment. An EU warehouse label also deserves clarification if the seller proposes moving the order through a different location.

For goods already in the EU at sale, the Commission describes a different customs position from goods dispatched into the EU from outside it. Confirm the transaction's actual route and VAT treatment in the written quote. [European Commission guidance on online purchases and import charges](https://taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu/customs/customs-procedures-import-and-export/importation/buying-goods-online-coming-non-european-union-country_en)

| Order detail | Ask for this confirmation | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dispatch | Country and current location of the confirmed unit | Establishes the route being priced |
| Product | Body, fitted head, material, skin tone and accessories | Avoids a configuration mismatch |
| Price | Currency, figure price, freight and tax treatment | Makes competing quotes comparable |
| Timing | Dispatch estimate and expected transport arrangements | Separates preparation from transit |
| Delivery | Carton dimensions, weight and service level | Helps plan access and lifting |
| Support | Seller contact and process for damage or faults | Identifies who handles the order afterwards |

If a quotation changes route after you approve it, request a revised breakdown. A different warehouse or made-to-order alternative can change the costs, documents and timing, even if the body height remains the same.

## Build a complete budget

The displayed USD figure price does not establish the amount you will spend to receive and set up the product. Ask for the confirmed body and head price, optional modules, packing or freight charges where applicable, and any amount payable to a carrier or authority. Record which entries are included, estimated or still awaiting confirmation.

For the current catalog, STPE companion prices are shown at $2,199 as of 15 November 2026. Silicone companions use suggested retail from a list dated 4 October 2025. The expression-head configuration is shown at $3,099 as of 15 November 2026. These are dated references to compare with a current written quote. The [shipping page](/shipping) explains how the site confirms freight and availability.

A practical budget can have two totals. The first is the amount payable to the seller. The second adds charges payable elsewhere and the home equipment you actually need, such as a suitable support stand. Ask your payment provider about exchange rates and fees if you pay in another currency. A rounded conversion in a blog article would not establish the amount charged on your statement.

For example, a lower figure price from a made-to-order route may become the more expensive option after freight, import charges and a support accessory. Compare both configurations with the same destination and required items. A quote with unanswered cost lines remains incomplete for a purchase decision.

## Confirm lawful supply to the destination

The store's adult-only scope and minimum height do not establish legality across every EU member state. Check any unresolved product or import question with the relevant national authority or a qualified local adviser. Give an accurate description of the adult appearance and any electronics, using the version you intend to order.

AIB Robot carries adult-realistic figures and excludes child-like styling and cartoon lines. We also decline inaccurate declarations or shipping arrangements intended to bypass a prohibition. Review the [adult products and destination-law guide](/blog/adult-products-and-the-law) before requesting delivery where the legal position is uncertain.

For the final invoice, ask for enough detail to connect the physical goods with the accepted configuration. Save it together with the order confirmation and any customs correspondence. If a document contains an error, request a correction through the seller or responsible carrier rather than editing a submitted official document yourself.

## Understand who the contract is with

Ask for the seller's identity, contact address, payment recipient and complaint channel. A fulfillment warehouse can pack an order without being the business responsible for selling it. Keep those roles clear in the written confirmation, especially when the dispatch country differs from the seller's location.

EU shopping rights may also apply when a non-EU trader targets EU consumers, although asserting them can be more difficult across borders. The relevant facts extend beyond where a carton is stored. [Your Europe guidance on consumer rights](https://europa.eu/youreurope/citizens/consumers/shopping/shopping-consumer-rights/index_en.htm)

Retain the dated description and promises on which you relied. For an expression head, this includes the supplied version, stated functions and any tested connection method. For a conventional figure, it includes the material, dimensions and head pairing. A lifestyle photograph can illustrate appearance, while the accepted specification records the product to be supplied.

## Check withdrawal and faulty-goods rights separately

Where EU distance-selling rules apply, online purchases generally have a 14-day withdrawal period. Exceptions can apply, including qualifying personalised goods and certain hygiene-sealed goods opened after delivery. Ask which conditions are relevant to the specific order before agreeing to them. A product being described as personal or intimate does not by itself answer every withdrawal question. [Your Europe returns guidance](https://europa.eu/youreurope/citizens/consumers/shopping/returns/index_en.htm) · [EU distance-selling business guidance](https://europa.eu/youreurope/business/selling-in-eu/selling-goods-services/ecommerce-distance-selling/index_en.htm)

Faults and misdescription raise separate rights. Your Europe explains the EU minimum two-year legal guarantee for goods that fail to conform, subject to the applicable rules and potentially longer national protection. This legal protection differs from an optional commercial warranty. [Your Europe guarantees guidance](https://europa.eu/youreurope/citizens/consumers/shopping/guarantees/index_en.htm)

Read the store's [returns policy](/returns) alongside those mandatory protections. Ask how damage is reported, which evidence is useful, and how any collection or return address will be agreed. Keep the packaging while an initial concern is being assessed. A defect report should describe the particular failure and identify the corresponding written specification.

## Prepare for the carton and the actual weight

Ask whether delivery is to the building entrance, a doorstep or another confirmed point. Establish access restrictions, stairs, lift dimensions and a safe indoor unpacking area. The catalog includes bodies listed at 45 kg and 51 kg. Packaging adds to the load, and a long carton can be awkward even when its weight seems manageable.

Arrange appropriate assistance or equipment before delivery. A stable padded surface should be ready so that you can inspect the figure without balancing it against furniture. Keep cutting tools away from the inner wrapping and preserve the labels and delivery paperwork. The [first-week guide](/blog/first-week-unboxing-and-setup) gives a staged inspection and setup workflow.

Plain outer packaging is requested and confirmed with an order. The carrier's tracking descriptions and handling labels are separate matters. Discuss delivery notifications and the recipient's availability if privacy in a shared home is important. Accurate paperwork and a discreet exterior can be planned together.

## Confirm the electrical and network configuration

For a voice module or expression head, obtain the supplied charger's input rating, approved output and connector details, plus the actual plug arrangement. A plug adapter changes the physical connection. It should not be treated as evidence that the electrical requirements match. Ask for the supplied version's instructions before connecting anything.

The expression-head figure's body is posed by hand. The head's current overseas Wi-Fi operation needs version-specific confirmation. Ask which functions can be demonstrated, what network conditions were used, and which services or accounts are required. A conventional body dispatched from Europe gives no additional evidence about the electronic head's compatibility.

The optional [AI voice box](/voice-box) is a separately described audio accessory. Identify it by name in the quote and request its own setup instructions. Keep its care and charging notes with the order file so that general body-cleaning guidance is not applied to electronic openings.

## Send a request that can be answered precisely

A useful inquiry provides the selected product, destination country and postcode, required material, preferred adult head pairing and optional electronics. Add practical constraints such as stairs, a maximum manageable weight or a need to compare warehouse and made-to-order options. Request an itemised quotation and a current availability check.

Before payment, compare the reply with that request line by line. Confirm any substitution, unresolved charge and timing condition in writing. Once the unit is accepted, retain those documents for delivery inspection and routine care. This gives you a clear basis for deciding whether the proposed EU purchase fits your budget, home and intended use.
