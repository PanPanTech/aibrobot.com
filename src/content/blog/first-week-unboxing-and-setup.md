The first week is inspection, cleaning, and a pose you can actually hold. It is not a photoshoot, and it is not the moment to discover you cannot lift the figure. Do the boring parts while the carton is still in the room.

## Before you cut the tape

Photograph the outer carton if a corner is crushed, a seam is split, or the box is wet. Then open it. Photograph the inner packing if it is loose or soaked. Then look at the figure. A tear, a detached finger, a head that will not sit, or an expression head with a dark screen are things you want on camera before the figure has been bathed, dressed, and moved three times. Write to us with those photographs. Do not ship the figure back until we have agreed an address and who pays the freight.

A change of mind is not in this paragraph. These are hygienic goods. A sound delivery that you decide you do not want is not a return. Read that before you order, not after the powder is on your floor.

## The first clean

Elastomer often arrives with a film from packing. Wash with a mild unscented cleaner, lukewarm water, and patience. Silicone gets a silicone-safe cleaner, not TPE oil. Rinse, pat dry, and powder an elastomer if it is tacky. This is also when you find marks that were hidden by the packing foam. Ordinary mould lines are not a defect. A split is. If you are unsure, a photograph is cheaper than a guess.

Keep the figure off a heater while it dries. A damp elastomer next to a radiator is how a surface warps.

## Posing

Pose slowly. The skeleton, where one is fitted, is there to hold a position, not to survive a yank. Support the torso. Seat the figure before you try a standing pose, and use a stand if you have one, especially above 40 kg. The 45 kg and 51 kg bodies in this catalog are not a one-person experiment on a staircase. Check fingers and toes after the first pose. A joint that clicked and now sits at a strange angle should be left alone until you have asked.

Clothing can wait a day. New dark dye against a freshly washed surface is a stain you will own. Light, washed clothing is the safer first outfit. The outfit in the product photograph is not required, and it may not even be included.

## If there is electronics

A voice box should be tried only after you have read the note that came with it, on a dry figure, away from the sink. An expression head should be charged the way its note says, not the way a phone is charged if the note disagrees. A unit that is dead on arrival is a defect. A head that cannot join a home network you were told we do not promise is the limit we already wrote down. If we confirmed a connection method in writing and the unit does not do that, write to us. If we did not confirm it, do not invent the confirmation after delivery.

By the end of the week you should have a clean figure in a stable pose, a note of anything that looked wrong, and the carton kept until you are sure you will not need it for a damage claim. That is a successful unboxing. The rest is ordinary care.
