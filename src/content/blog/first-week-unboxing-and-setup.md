Use the first week to document the delivered condition, confirm the configuration, establish a manageable cleaning routine and find a stable supported pose. Test optional electronics with their own instructions on a dry figure. Arrange the lifting and resting area before opening the carton so that inspection can proceed without rushed handling.

This workflow applies to the adult-realistic collection. The current expression-head figure has a body posed by hand, with limited powered head and facial movement. Its overseas home Wi-Fi operation needs version-specific confirmation. The routine below lets you separate transport condition, body setup and electronic checks while retaining the evidence relevant to each.

## Prepare the room before delivery

Choose an unpacking area with enough space for the carton and a clean padded support. Remove sharp objects and clear the route from the delivery point. Check stairs, door widths and lift access in advance. Find out where the carrier's agreed service ends so that you can arrange any further movement yourself.

Use the actual confirmed weight when planning assistance. The catalog includes 45 kg and 51 kg bodies, and packaging adds weight and awkward dimensions. A narrow staircase can make a long carton difficult even when several people are available. Ask about carton measurements before dispatch if access is uncertain.

Gather a camera, the order confirmation, soft cloths and the approved care instructions. Keep cutting tools where they cannot fall onto the figure. Set aside a place for protective packing and small accessories, then read the [shipping information](/shipping) and [returns process](/returns) so you know what to retain if a problem appears.

## Day one begins with photographs and an inventory

Photograph the outer carton before moving or opening it further, especially if it is crushed, split or wet. Include the shipment label in a private record. Open the box carefully with the cutting edge kept away from the inner contents. Lift packing away in stages and record any displaced supports or damaged areas.

Compare the delivered items with the written order. Check the body, head pairing, material confirmation, wig, eyes and separately ordered accessories. A photographed outfit may have been collection styling, so use the agreed packing list to establish what should be included. Keep small connectors and instruction sheets together.

| Check | What to record | Suitable next action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Outer carton | Damage, labels and delivery condition | Photograph and retain the packaging |
| Protective packing | Loose supports, moisture or crushed areas | Record before removing further layers |
| Body and head | Visible marks, seams and confirmed pairing | Compare with the accepted description |
| Accessories | Actual items and their labels | Match them to the written list |
| Power equipment | Charger label, connector and instructions | Confirm compatibility before connecting |
| Electronics | Version, controls and any visible damage | Follow the documented dry setup sequence |

Photographs should include one view showing where an issue is and another showing its detail. Record a brief description and the order reference. Send the report through the agreed private contact process. Avoid posting addresses, identity details or full shipping labels in a public review.

## Pause when the initial condition needs assessment

If you find a tear, an opening seam, a damaged port or a component that differs from the order, stop the activity that could affect that area. Keep the packing and request guidance. Repeatedly flexing a suspect joint or cleaning over a mark can make the original condition harder to assess.

A dark electronic display is a symptom to document. The next step depends on the supplied setup instructions and approved power equipment. Follow only the basic checks appropriate to the unit, then report the outcome if it still fails. Avoid opening the head, modifying a charger or applying pressure to a moving facial part.

The store assesses arrival damage, missing items and suspected faults through its returns process. Applicable consumer protections may also be relevant. Arrange any return address and transport method before sending a heavy figure back. A support report preserves the ability to consider the problem without adding avoidable transport or handling complications.

## Make the first clean material-specific

After inspection, confirm which surfaces are TPE, STPE or silicone. A mixed-material head and body need different treatment where their instructions differ. Prepare the cleaner named in the care sheet and follow its dilution and removal directions. Work in sections while keeping the rest of the figure supported.

Use gentle cleaning and controlled rinsing. Keep liquid away from seams leading into electronics, speakers, ports, displays and attachment areas that must stay dry. Do not immerse an electronic head. Allow the cleaned body to dry away from direct sunlight or a heater, and check folds and contact areas before adding clothing.

Cleaning and disinfection have different requirements. EPA explains that disinfectants have specific label directions and contact times. Those directions still need to be compatible with the finished figure. A household spray's strength does not establish that it is appropriate for the material or paint. [EPA explanation of cleaning and disinfection](https://www.epa.gov/coronavirus-and-disinfectants/whats-difference-between-products-disinfect-sanitize-and-clean)

Use the [TPE and STPE guide](/blog/caring-for-tpe-and-stpe) or the [silicone guide](/blog/silicone-doll-care-and-longevity) for the next steps. Add powder or renewal products only where the exact unit's instructions recommend them.

## Establish a supported pose before dressing

Move slowly and support the torso and limb being repositioned. Keep joints within the range described for the figure. Stop if resistance changes unexpectedly, a joint gives way or the surface becomes trapped. Fingers, toes and hair are poor lifting points for the body's weight.

Start with a supported resting or seated position that lets you inspect the contact areas. If you plan to stand the figure, confirm the body options and stand's capacity and fitting method first. The current bodies are manually posed. A photograph of a standing figure cannot establish the support arrangement behind that photograph.

Choose light, colour-fast clothing for the first outfit and inspect its inside seams and fasteners. Remove accessories with sharp edges before repositioning. Check the surface when changing outfits, especially where elastic or dark trim was in contact. The [posing and storage article](/blog/posing-stands-and-storage) covers long-term support choices.

## Check optional electronics as a separate task

Once the figure and work area are dry, read the supplied electronic guide. Verify the approved charger, input and output information and connector before use. Keep the charger and its instructions associated with the unit. CPSC describes batteries and chargers as a system whose compatibility matters to safety. [CPSC information on batteries and chargers](https://www.cpsc.gov/Regulations-Laws--Standards/Voluntary-Standards/Topics/Batteries)

Test one documented function at a time. Record the software or hardware version and any error message. For a voice box, use its own controls and setup notes. For an expression head, use the confirmed operating procedure and avoid forcing the face or neck by hand while a powered mechanism is active.

If connection fails, note the method attempted and the precise error. Compare it with the version-specific guidance confirmed before dispatch. The current product page does not establish reliable operation on a US or EU home network. Do not install an unofficial application or alter internal wiring to troubleshoot a newly delivered unit.

## Use the remaining days to refine the routine

Once the basic checks are complete, observe how the support, clothing and storage location work in practice. Inspect pressure areas after the first pose and make a gentle adjustment if needed. Store cloths, care products and electronics instructions together so that future maintenance is repeatable.

A useful end-of-week record contains the accepted configuration, delivery photographs, accessory inventory, care instructions and the outcome of electronic checks. Keep any unresolved concern in the same conversation with the order reference. The [buyer checklist](/blog/buyers-checklist-before-you-order) remains useful for verifying an omitted detail, while the [care page](/care) provides the routine to follow once setup is complete.
