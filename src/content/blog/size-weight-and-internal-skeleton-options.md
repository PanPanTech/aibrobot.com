The outside of a companion figure is what the photograph shows. The inside is a skeleton, a filler, and a skin, and the inside is why a pose holds or fails. We will describe the pieces we have actually seen noted. We will not invent a joint count.

## Size and weight together

Height without weight is a costume size. A 157 cm body at 29 kg and a 163 cm body at 51 kg are not neighbours in your arms, even though the centimetres look close. Read both numbers on the product page. Where a measurement is missing — a foot length, an under bust, a shoulder — it is missing because the row we copied did not have it. Ask us to confirm it for the unit. Do not average it from a different body.

The expression-head figure is 164 cm and F-cup in the notes we are using. It does not have a full chart or a weight on this site. We confirm those before dispatch. We do not paste the 164 cm companion body’s 33 kg and its bust measurements onto the expression head. They are different products, and one of the photographs shared between them even carries an overlay with a different height. The table, not the overlay, is the listing.

## Skeleton and hands

A note in the material we have describes a ball-jointed hand skeleton and related structure in the limbs. That is as specific as we will be. It means fingers are meant to be posed, not wrenched, and that a hand can be a separate option. On the silicone companions, a 4 October 2025 sheet lists an updated hand as a free option. “Free on a sheet” still has to be confirmed as available on the unit you would receive. STPE companions are listed as bodies with a separately chosen head. Hands, if a change is possible, are part of that confirmation, not a checkbox we have printed as guaranteed.

Standing feet, where the silicone sheet lists them beside normal feet, are an option for a figure you intend to stand. They are not a promise that the figure balances like a person. Use a stand. The skeleton holds a pose. It does not fight gravity on a slippery floor.

## What we will not add

We will not publish a degree-of-freedom number, a load rating for a joint, or a claim that the skeleton makes the figure walk. Companion bodies are posed by hand. The expression head has limited motion in the head and neck and none in the sense of taking a step. If you need a machine that relocates itself, this catalog does not contain it.

When you order, say the pose you actually want: seated most of the time, or standing with a stand, or standing feet if they can be confirmed. Say it because the skeleton is configured and then left alone. The first week is when you learn its range. After that, you stay inside the range that did not click, pinch, or crease the skin. That is the whole skill, and it is enough.
