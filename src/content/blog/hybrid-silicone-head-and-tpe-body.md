A hybrid, in this category, usually means a silicone head on a TPE or STPE body. People want it because they like the face of one material and the cost or the feel of the other. Sometimes that configuration is a real option. Sometimes it is a photograph of two parts that were never meant to meet. The difference is the neck, and the neck is not something to assume.

## What has to match

A head sits on a neck opening with a particular diameter, a joint, and sometimes a bolt or a sleeve. Two heads that look similar in a front photograph can have different hardware behind the jaw. Two bodies of the same height can have different neck cups. If you ask for a hybrid, the question we have to answer is whether that head is supplied for that body, not whether the face is pretty. We confirm it before payment. We will not tell you to force a fit.

Care splits in two as well. The body, if it is TPE or STPE, wants the elastomer routine: mild wash, powder, caution with dye and heat. The head, if it is silicone, wants a silicone-safe cleaner and does not want a puddle of TPE oil worked into the hairline. Makeup and wig glue sit on the head. Keep them off the neck joint, where they become grit.

## What a hybrid is not

It is not the expression-head figure. That product is a specific 164 cm F-cup body with head #1068, listed at $3,099, with a display and a network question. Putting a silicone face on a companion body does not create that product, and it does not make either part walk.

It is also not a way to mix every option on the 2025 silicone sheet with every STPE body in the shop. Implanted hair, eye colour, and the free updated-hand note belong to the configurations where they were listed. We confirm each one. A hybrid that needs a custom neck piece may not be available on the timeline of a warehouse body. If the body is on a November 2026 list, the head on that unit is the head we can see, unless a change is confirmed as possible. “Available to order” is where unusual pairings belong, and even then only if we can actually supply them.

## How to ask

Name the body from its product page, name the head you want as clearly as you can, and say whether you need the head in silicone. Send the question before you add a confident configuration to the inquiry bag. If we cannot confirm the neck, the answer is the standard head for that body, or no order. A failed hybrid is a figure with a head that rocks, pinches, or will not stay. That is a worse result than a matched pair you liked slightly less in a photograph.

The sample head in a pack shot is already a kind of pairing. It shows that a head can sit there. It does not show that every other head can. Ask.
