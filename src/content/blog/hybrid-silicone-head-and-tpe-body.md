A hybrid companion pairs a silicone head with a TPE or STPE body. Choose one only after the exact head, neck connection, skin tone, and body have been confirmed together. The appeal is straightforward. You may prefer a particular silicone face while wanting the proportions or care characteristics of an elastomer body.

The connection determines whether that preference becomes a usable configuration. Similar-looking heads and bodies can have different fittings, clearances, or intended assembly methods. Send the body page and head reference in the same inquiry, and ask which pairing can be supplied as a complete confirmed order.

## Check the complete pairing

| Detail | Why it matters | What to request |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Head reference and material | Identifies the face and its care requirements | Exact head identifier and silicone confirmation |
| Body reference and material | Defines dimensions, weight, and maintenance | Exact body identifier and TPE or STPE confirmation |
| Neck interface | Determines assembly and support | Confirmation that the connection is compatible and instructions for use |
| Color and finish | Affects the visible transition at the neck | Current images of the offered pairing where available |
| Head weight and assembly | Affects handling and neck loading | Approved fitting and removal method, if removable |
| Hair and eyes | Changes care and included parts | Fitted hair type, eye option, and accessories |
| Electronics | Adds control, power, and maintenance requirements | Exact module and complete configuration confirmation |

## Start with a supplied configuration

The simplest starting point is a head and body that can be confirmed as a supplied pair. A seller's ability to show a similar combination is helpful, but the order still needs a written identification of both components.

If a warehouse unit already exists, ask which head is fitted and whether the requested pairing would change that unit. A photograph may belong to an earlier sample configuration. Current confirmation should identify what will actually be dispatched.

A custom pairing introduces questions about assembly, color, available components, and timing. Send your preferred result before treating it as an accepted option. The [warehouse and made-to-order guide](/blog/in-stock-vs-made-to-order) explains why stock status applies to a specific configuration.

Avoid ordering two loose parts on the assumption that a connector can be improvised later. A complete pairing gives you a clear basis for inspection, support, and care.

## Ask how the neck connection works

A neck interface can involve a threaded fitting, joint, sleeve, or another design. Its visible outline in a photograph gives limited information about how the parts engage and support the head.

Ask whether any adapter is included, how the head is installed, and whether removal is intended for the owner. If assembly is required, request the instructions before attempting it. A compatible connection should hold the head as intended without forcing the surrounding skin into position.

Also ask about movement limits. A head that rotates on one body's fitting may behave differently on another. Weight, clearance under the jaw, and the neck opening all matter to the complete assembly.

If a fitting binds, rocks, or leaves an unexpected gap during setup, stop and photograph the issue. Apply the [inspection and returns process](/returns) before modifying parts. Grinding, gluing, or forcing a connector can make the original condition difficult to assess.

## Confirm color with realistic expectations

Silicone and elastomer can respond differently to lighting and finish. A skin-tone name can be useful within a range, while the visible head-to-body transition still depends on the particular pairing.

Ask for a photograph of the intended combination where one is available, ideally with the neck visible in even lighting. Keep the image as a configuration reference. Screen brightness and photography still limit how precisely you can judge a color match remotely.

A high collar, scarf, or wig can conceal the neck in a promotional image. If the transition matters to you, request an unobstructed view before ordering. The [photo-reading guide](/blog/reading-photographs-and-overlays) explains how to use sample images alongside the specification.

Record any agreed color and finish in the quotation. This is especially useful when a named head is available in several tones or when an older photograph shows a different sample body.

## Give each material its own care routine

A hybrid has two surfaces and potentially two sets of care instructions. Identify which cleaner, powder, or renewal product belongs on each component and keep that information with the order.

For the elastomer body, follow its approved washing, drying, and finishing guidance. For the silicone head, use instructions appropriate to the silicone, paint, hair attachment, and eyes. Keep body treatments away from the face and neck fitting while you work.

Material formulation matters beyond the label. Polymer manufacturer Avient describes TPE as a family of materials with application-specific formulations. That is useful context for requesting the finished figure's own care guidance. [Avient's material introduction](https://www.avient.com/resource-center/knowledge-base/article/beginner-s-guide-tpe)

Use clean cloths for each task. Support the figure in a stable position before cleaning around the neck, and avoid letting liquid collect at an opening or fitting. The [general care page](/care) and [silicone care article](/blog/silicone-doll-care-and-longevity) provide the next steps.

## Plan hair, eyes, and clothing together

A removable wig and implanted hair create different routines. Ask which is fitted to the quoted head, whether the pictured wig is included, and how the hair should be handled during assembly.

Implanted hair should be supported at the roots during gentle detangling. Wig care can often be performed separately once removal is confirmed. Follow the instructions for the actual fiber and cap construction.

Collars, necklaces, and zippers touch the neck transition. Choose clothing that can be fitted without dragging a sharp edge across either material. Avoid tightening an accessory to disguise an assembly problem.

Keep cosmetics, adhesives, powder, and oil away from the connection. The [hair and makeup guide](/blog/wigs-makeup-and-surface-aftercare) addresses the surfaces around the face in more detail.

## Identify any electronic head by its own model

The current expression-head figure has a dedicated [164 cm #1068 listing](/ai-robots/wm-164-f-1068). Its moving functions, control, power requirements, and body-material confirmation belong to that complete configuration.

If you want electronic expressions, request the exact head version and the offered body together. A conventional silicone head's compatibility with a body does not establish compatibility for an electronic assembly.

Also distinguish a requested [voice box](/voice-box) from the head assembly. Ask where it is fitted, which functions are included, and how care around its opening is performed. Record each component separately so the quotation describes the features you intended to buy.

## Example of a useful pairing request

Suppose you prefer a silicone head shown in one collection photograph and the dimensions of a 166 cm STPE body. Send both references and state that you want the body in its listed STPE material.

Ask whether that exact head can be supplied on the body, how the connection is assembled, whether an adapter is required, and which head weight and total dimensions apply. Add your preferred hair option and destination country.

Request a current image of the proposed pairing where available and a complete quotation identifying the fitted components. If the desired pairing cannot be supplied, compare a confirmed alternative against the same measurements and care requirements.

## Inspect the pairing before styling it

During unboxing, compare the head and body references with the written confirmation. Photograph the connection and inspect the surrounding skin before attaching accessories or applying products.

Follow the assembly instructions slowly, with the figure supported. Check that the head rests and moves as the instructions describe. Keep the supplied packing while an unexpected fitting or color question is being assessed.

Once the pairing is confirmed, keep the component instructions together. A well-documented hybrid is easier to maintain because you know what each surface and connection requires.
