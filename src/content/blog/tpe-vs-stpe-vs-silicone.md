A companion figure's material determines how its surface feels, which cleaning products belong on it, and how much everyday care fits your routine. Start by identifying the material of the exact head and body you would receive. Then compare maintenance, handling weight, clothing, and the complete quotation. A photograph alone cannot settle those questions.

The current AIB Robot collection includes silicone companions and bodies listed in STPE. TPE builds can be requested for some STPE-listed bodies and are quoted after availability is confirmed. These distinctions matter before a deposit, even when two figures share a height or face.

## Understand what TPE tells you

TPE means thermoplastic elastomer. It describes a broad family of flexible materials whose formulation changes their feel and performance. A polymer manufacturer's explanation helps with the terminology, while the care sheet for a finished figure determines how you should treat that product. Avient describes the distinction between thermoplastic elastomers and cured thermoset rubbers in its materials FAQ. [Avient's TPE explanation](https://www.avient.com/resources/knowledge-base/article/thermoplastic-elastomer-tpe-faqs)

For a buyer, the important consequence is variation. Two figures advertised as TPE may differ in softness, surface finish, internal filling, and how their skin behaves around joints. A material label provides a starting point for questions. It cannot provide a guaranteed feel from a screen.

The TPE care notes used for this collection call for mild, unscented cleaning or a product specifically recommended for the material, complete drying, and a light application of the recommended renewal powder when the surface is tacky. Renewal oil, where recommended, is a separate step for the condition identified in that care guidance. Adding more oil simply because a surface feels unusual can make the diagnosis harder.

Ask for current instructions with your quotation. Keep those instructions with the order confirmation so you can refer to the same material and version later. The [elastomer care guide](/blog/caring-for-tpe-and-stpe) covers the routine in more detail.

## Read STPE claims against the actual product

STPE is the label used for the elastomer bodies listed in this collection. Notes supplied with them describe reduced oiling, low odor, and improved durability compared with standard TPE. We have not performed comparative laboratory tests on those claims. A buyer should ask what the particular claim covers and whether any test report refers to the exact finished material.

Spelling can be confusing. A short sequence of letters in a product description does not establish a universally shared recipe across every manufacturer. Record the full material description in the quotation, including whether it applies to the head, body, or both. When comparing sellers, check that they are discussing the same configuration before comparing their claims.

Daily use still involves cleaning, drying, clothing changes, support, and checking contact areas. Less visible oil would simplify one part of that routine. It would not remove the effects of pressure, sharp accessories, damp fabric, or an unsupported heavy body. Choose STPE only if the full elastomer routine is something you are prepared to maintain.

A useful question is what changes in the supplied care instructions when you choose STPE instead of a requested TPE build. An answer about actual cleaning and maintenance is more actionable than a broad promise of a longer life.

## What changes when you choose silicone

Silicone companions in this catalog use a separate set of listed bodies and measurements. Their care follows products intended for silicone, with attention to the painted finish, hair attachment, eyes, and joints. A silicone label does not make every cleaner suitable for the face or every oil appropriate for the body.

Surface feel is personal. A smooth portrait photograph communicates sculpt and lighting more readily than softness or friction. If you want a particular feel, describe it in your inquiry and request current information about the specific body. Ask whether the head and body have the same material and whether the photographed finish corresponds to the offered unit.

Silicone is commonly chosen by buyers who prefer its maintenance characteristics and available sculpts. Even so, clothing can transfer color, concentrated pressure can mark a soft surface, and careless handling can damage a joint or seam. Plan storage and support with the same care you would apply to an elastomer figure.

## Separate material from weight and construction

The same material can appear on bodies with very different proportions. In the current catalog, a 157 cm B-cup STPE companion is listed at 29 kg, while the 163 cm H-cup body is listed at 51 kg. Those two figures create substantially different handling tasks even though the taller one is only six centimeters higher.

Weight also reflects the internal frame, body volume, and construction. It should come from the correct product specification. Using a material's reputation to predict the weight of an unlisted configuration risks choosing a body that will be difficult to move.

Write down the net weight, the packaged weight when available, and the intended support arrangement. An electronic head or a changed body option may change what needs to be confirmed. The [height and weight guide](/blog/how-to-choose-height-cup-and-weight) explains how to compare real dimensions, while the [skeleton guide](/blog/size-weight-and-internal-skeleton-options) covers internal support.

## Compare the head and body separately

A silicone head on a TPE or STPE body is a hybrid configuration. It may offer the face you prefer with a different body material. That combination must be confirmed for the exact neck connection, color, and head weight.

Care follows the component. A body renewal product can reach the neck during handling, so keep the head protected while treating the body. Hair adhesive, cosmetics, and cleaners also need to be compatible with the surface they touch. One bottle advertised for a broad category should not be assumed suitable for every attached component.

If you are considering a hybrid, send the body page and the desired head reference together. Ask which head arrives fitted, which parts are removable, and whether instructions cover separation and reassembly. Read the [hybrid head and body guide](/blog/hybrid-silicone-head-and-tpe-body) before ordering loose parts.

## Evaluate durability without inventing a lifespan

A lifespan estimate is only helpful when the conditions are stated. Daily handling, clothing dyes, storage temperature, joint loading, cleaning products, and the painted finish all affect the owner's experience. A material comparison cannot turn those variables into a reliable number of years.

If a seller quotes a multiple of another material's durability, ask which property was measured. Tear strength, abrasion, color transfer, and visible oiling describe different outcomes. Also ask whether the comparison concerns a material sample or a completed figure with joints, paint, and hair.

For your own records, photograph the surface at arrival in even light. Keep the approved product names and note any change before applying a new treatment. This gives aftercare support something useful to assess if a patch becomes sticky, discolored, or damaged.

The order's written defect and remedy terms remain important alongside material choice. Review the [returns process](/returns) before beginning a repair, and send photographs when a new issue needs assessment.

## What to send with your inquiry

Send links to the two bodies you are considering, the head reference, destination country, preferred material, and any standing or hair option you need. Add a short description of how you will use and store the figure, especially if the available space or lifting arrangement is limited.

Ask for the material of each component, confirmed net weight, supplied care instructions, included accessories, and complete price. If any description conflicts with an image overlay or an older note, identify it explicitly so it can be resolved in the order confirmation.
