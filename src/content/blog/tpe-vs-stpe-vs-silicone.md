## TPE

Thermoplastic elastomer is soft and slightly tacky until it is powdered. It is widely used because it is forgiving to the touch and less expensive to produce than silicone. It also oils. Over time a TPE surface can look wet, pick up lint, and stain from dark clothing. Heat, sun, and a hot car are unkind to it. Care is ordinary and repetitive: a mild unscented wash, a full rinse, a pat dry, and a light powder. A little renewal oil can help a dry patch. More oil is not better. Solvents, alcohol, and petroleum jelly are a good way to ruin the surface.

We do not publish a standing TPE price for the warehouse bodies in this collection. If you want TPE instead of the STPE listing, ask, and we price that build when we know it can be made.

## STPE

STPE is the elastomer in which those $2,199 companions are listed. Notes supplied with the material describe less oiling than standard TPE, little odor, and greater durability, sometimes framed as a multiple of TPE’s life. That is a claim in the notes. We have not laboratory-tested it, and we will not dress it up as our own measurement. What we can say is practical: treat STPE with the same respect you would give TPE until your own unit teaches you otherwise. Keep it cool, powdered if it is tacky, and away from unstable dyes. If it oils less, you will notice. If it does not, you were not sold a laboratory certificate.

## Silicone

Silicone generally oils less and feels different under the hand. It is usually cleaned with a product meant for silicone, not with TPE oil. It marks less easily from some dyes and is often described as longer-lived, which is a fair generalisation and still not a warranty for a particular seam. Silicone heads on softer bodies are a separate configuration, discussed in the hybrid article. Do not assume a silicone head can be swapped onto every body, and do not assume the neck hardware matches.

The expression-head figure’s material disagreement matters more than a preference. If you are paying $3,099 for a head on a body, you should know which skin you are receiving. We will say so before you pay. We will not guess from a title that disagrees with another note.

## How to choose without a myth

Choose silicone if you want the surface that usually needs less powder and you accept the 2025 suggested retail on those bodies. Choose the STPE listing if you want the current $2,199 price and you are willing to care for an elastomer, with the durability claim left as a claim. Request TPE only if you specifically want that older feel and you are prepared for a price that is confirmed later, not printed here.

None of the three materials walks. None of them makes a companion body into the expression head. Photographs, including ones with a printed overlay, are not a material test. The specification table is the listing. If a photo and the table disagree, believe the table and ask us to confirm the unit.
