The phrase “AI bionic robot” is doing too much work. In a video it can mean a machine that walks across a room, holds a conversation, and keeps a steady temperature. On this site it means something narrower, and the narrower meaning is the one you can order.

AIB Robot is a category site for AI bionic and companion robots. The figure in the catalog with an expression head is a 164 cm F-cup body with head #1068, listed at $3,099 as of 15 November 2026. It is available to order. It is not on the November 2026 US or EU warehouse lists. A companion body of a similar height being on a warehouse list does not put the expression head in stock.

## What the head is described as doing

Notes supplied with this head describe a voice platform, a touch display of about four inches, a microphone and speakers, Wi-Fi and Bluetooth, and facial expressions that include a smile, a made face, and a kiss. The same notes mention language switching, stories, weather, reminders, and saved questions. They also give about eight hours of standby and about three hours of continuous interaction. Those times are in the notes. We have not timed a unit ourselves, and we will not turn them into a promise.

Material is not settled on the page until we confirm a unit. Some notes title the body as silicone. Others describe an STPE body with this head. If you inquire, that is one of the items we confirm before payment, along with the price.

## What it does not do

It does not walk. The body does not move on its own. You pose it by hand, and the motion that is described is limited to the head and neck. If a listing anywhere tells you this class of product will cross a room, climb stairs, or behave like a research humanoid, that listing is describing a different product or it is describing a wish.

It also does not come with a promise about your home network. Notes we have seen describe trouble on networks away from where the head was set up, and the head’s own instructions tell the user to check the network if none is found. We do not promise that facial expressions and conversation features already work on a typical US or EU home Wi-Fi network. Connectivity is confirmed before dispatch. If reliable daily use on your router is the reason you want the product, wait, or ask us what can be confirmed for the unit you would actually receive.

We also do not borrow feature lists from other companies in this broad category. You will not find a degree-of-freedom count, a constant body temperature, or a named conversational model on this page, because those are not claims we are prepared to make about this head.

## How it sits next to a companion body

The rest of the catalog is adult companion figures, 157 cm and above, in silicone or STPE. They are transitional products. They are what a buyer can choose while the “robot” side of the category is still a head on a body rather than a mobile machine. They do not walk either. A voice box, offered at $100 pending confirmation, is a third thing: audio in the body, not an expression head, and not dependent on that Wi-Fi question.

A sensible comparison is therefore three prices and three jobs. A silicone companion at the 2025 suggested retail of $1,900 or $1,950 is a body and a head you pose and care for. An STPE companion at $2,199, shown beside a previous list price of $2,599, is the same kind of object in a different material. The expression-head figure at $3,099 adds a networked face and a list of limits. If you want the face, budget for the limits. If you want a quiet figure with a care routine and no network, the companion bodies are the honest purchase.

## What to ask before you order

Ask which configuration is being offered, not a category name. Ask the current price and whether it includes the expression head, packing, and any support that has actually been described. Ask whether the unit is on a warehouse list or has to be made, and treat any timing as an estimate until it is written for your order. Ask what happens if a motor, a sensor, or the display fails. Ask how to clean the head without soaking electronics. And ask, in plain words, whether anyone is promising that it walks or that it will join your home network. The correct answers, for the figure we list, are no and “we confirm connectivity before dispatch.”

This category is real enough to have a height, a cup, a head number, and a price. It is unfinished enough, on overseas networks, that the unfinished part belongs in the product description. Buy it as an early expression head on a posed adult body, or buy a companion figure and a voice box, or wait. Do not buy it as a walking household robot. That product is not the one on this site.
