## Separate appearance, movement and conversation

A realistic face can be attractive while the range of movement remains limited. Decide which visible actions matter to you. For example, someone who wants occasional facial expressions while a figure sits nearby has a different requirement from someone who expects continuous eye contact during an unscripted conversation.

Use a short description of the situation you want to create. You might write that the figure will remain supported in a seated position, that conversation in English is essential, and that you want to understand which expressions can be selected. That description gives the seller something specific to answer. Terms such as intelligent, lifelike or emotional are too broad to establish the operating behavior.

The body needs manual positioning and support. Standing feet, where separately available on a companion figure, concern the physical support arrangement. They establish no independent balancing or walking capability. Before buying, consider how you will lift the figure, place it safely and access the head's controls without pulling on delicate parts.
