TPE and STPE are soft elastomers. They reward a boring routine and they punish heat, dye, and neglect. The STPE companions in this catalog are the bodies listed at $2,199. A TPE build can be requested and is priced on confirmation. Notes about STPE describe less oiling and greater durability than standard TPE. We have not laboratory-tested that claim. Care for both as elastomers until your unit shows you otherwise. If a care sheet arrives in the carton, that sheet wins.

## Washing

Wash your hands first. Support the head and the joints. Do not lift the figure by a wrist, an ankle, or the hair. Use a mild unscented soap or a cleaner meant for this material, and warm water that is not hot. Rinse until the surface is not slippery with soap. Pat dry with a soft cloth. Do not wring the material, and do not put the figure in a bath and walk away. Standing water finds seams.

If the figure has a voice box, keep water out of the module, the port, and the speaker. An expression head, if you have one on another product, is not a TPE part you can soak. This article is about the body.

## Powder and oil

Elastomer is often tacky after it dries. A thin dusting of cornstarch-based powder, or a powder the care notes recommend, is the usual finish. Put it on lightly and brush off the excess. If a patch looks dry, a small amount of renewal oil meant for that material can help. Pools of oil collect dust and can stain cloth. Keep oil and powder away from eyes, from any glue line under a wig, and from electronics.

Oiling is normal for TPE. It can look alarming the first time and still be ordinary. A tear, a split seam, or a colour that wiped off onto your hands in a way the care notes do not describe is not ordinary. Photograph it and write to us before you attempt a repair.

## Heat, dye, and pose

Direct sun, a heater, and a closed car will damage elastomer. Dark or cheap dyes can leave a permanent shadow in the shape of a garment. Light, colour-fast clothing is safer. Do not store the figure folded sharply for weeks. A supported seat or a stand is kinder than a heap. The heavy bodies in this collection, at 45 kg and 51 kg, need a stand that is meant for the weight, not a decorative tripod you have not checked.

## Storage

A cool, dry room is enough. A breathable cover is better than a sealed plastic bag for a long pause. If other people share the home and you want privacy, the cover is also the discreet option. Powder again when you bring the figure back out, and check for flat spots where it rested.

None of this turns the figure into a robot, and none of it is optional if you want the surface you saw in the photograph to last past the first month. The photograph was taken on a good day. The care is how you get a second month.
