Silicone companions in this catalog are the bodies listed at a suggested retail of $1,900 or $1,950 on a 4 October 2025 price list. People choose them because the surface usually oils less than TPE and feels different in the hand. That is a fair reason. It is not a promise that a particular figure will look new for a decade, and it is not a reason to clean it with whatever is under the sink.

## Cleaning

Use a cleaner meant for silicone, not a solvent, not alcohol, not acetone, and not a TPE oil you already own. Wash with warm water that is not hot, rinse thoroughly, and pat dry. Support the head and joints while you move the figure. Do not lift it by the hair or by a single limb. The weights on these silicone bodies run from the low thirties to about 39 kg. That is still a lift you should plan.

Keep water out of the eyes if they are removable, and out of any voice-box hardware. The $30 sound option and the $100 voice box are both electronics living in a figure you are washing. Treat the openings as openings.

## What ages the surface

Sun, heat, and a closed car will mark silicone as they mark everything else in this category, even if the failure looks different from TPE oiling. Sharp edges, jewellery, and rough nails will cut it. Unstable clothing dye is less famous on silicone than on TPE and still not worth testing with a new black dress on the first day. Makeup, if you add it, should be a product you can remove with the same gentle cleaner. Pigment that was not meant for this surface becomes a stain you own.

Joints and the neck see more movement than the torso. If a seam opens or a finger joint goes soft in a way that feels structural, stop posing that limb and photograph it. A repair you invent can turn a defect we could have assessed into damage that happened after delivery.

## Longevity, stated modestly

Silicone is generally more stable against oiling than TPE. That is as far as we will go. We do not publish a year count. Storage in a cool, dry place, a breathable cover, and a habit of not folding the figure for months will do more for the surface than any sentence on a sales page. If you rotate wigs and clothing, you also rotate the stress on the neck and the dye risk. Both are worth the extra minute.

The expression-head figure may or may not be silicone. Some notes say it is; others describe an STPE body with the head. Do not apply this article to that product until we have confirmed the material of the unit. An expression head is also not a silicone part you immerse. Its care is the body’s care plus a dry rule for the display, the ports, and the speakers.

A silicone companion is a transitional product in this catalog: an adult figure you pose and maintain, not a walking robot. The care is how you keep it a figure you still want to look at. The photograph was the first day. The cleaner is the rest.
