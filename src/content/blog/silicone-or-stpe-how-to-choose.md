Two companion lines sit side by side in this shop, and the useful difference is material, measurement, and price — not a story about which name is secretly better. One line is silicone, at a suggested retail of $1,900 or $1,950 from a 4 October 2025 list. The other is STPE, at $2,199 beside a previous list price of $2,599 as of 15 November 2026. Both are adult figures, 157 cm and above. Neither walks. Both are transitional products next to the longer-term idea of an AI bionic robot.

## Start with the room, not the label

A 175 cm figure at 38 kg is a different object from a 157 cm figure at 29 kg, even when the prices look similar. Measure the doorway, the closet, and the place it will sit. A heavy figure — this catalog includes bodies at 45 kg and 51 kg — needs a plan for lifting and a stand that can carry the weight. If you cannot move it safely, the material is the wrong question.

Then read the measurement table. Full bust, under bust, waist, hip, shoulder, and limb lengths are printed only where we have them. A missing number is omitted on purpose. Do not fill it in from a photograph. Some photographs carry a printed overlay that names a height or a head. That overlay is not our title. The table is.

## Then the surface

Silicone usually oils less and wants silicone-safe cleaner rather than TPE oil. STPE is an elastomer. Notes about it describe less oiling and more durability than standard TPE. We have not laboratory-tested that description. If you dislike tackiness and powder, silicone is the simpler daily surface. If you want the current STPE price and you will keep a care routine, the STPE listing is the straightforward one. A TPE build can be requested on an STPE-listed body and is priced only after confirmation. It will feel softer and it will need more powder. That is a preference, not a discount.

Heads are chosen with the order. A head you like in a photograph may be an example pairing, not the head on the unit in a warehouse. Ask. Neck fit is not something to invent after the carton arrives.

## Price is not a rank

The silicone suggested retail and the STPE current price are different kinds of number, taken from different dates. A higher number is not a certificate of a better sculpt, and a lower number is not a clearance of a worse one. Compare a complete configuration: height, cup if one is listed, material, weight, whether the body is on a November 2026 warehouse list, and what the photograph actually shows. Warehouse presence is reconfirmed before payment. We do not publish how many units are on a shelf.

The expression-head figure, at $3,099, is not a “better” version of either companion line. It is a head with a display and a network dependency, on a 164 cm F-cup body, and it does not walk. If you are choosing a companion body, leave that product out of the comparison unless you specifically want the face to move.

## A way to decide

Write two columns. On the left, the constraints: height you can house, weight you can lift, material you will actually clean, destination we can ship to lawfully. On the right, the figures that survive those constraints. If only one remains, you are done. If two remain and you prefer one face, choose the face and let us confirm the head. If you are choosing because a forum ranked the lines, close the forum. Taste is allowed. A rank we did not publish is not a specification.

AIB Robot will not misdeclare a shipment to make either material easier to import. If the destination does not allow the product, the comparison ends there.
