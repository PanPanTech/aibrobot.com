Choose between silicone and STPE by comparing two specific bodies that fit your space, handling arrangements, preferred face, and care routine. Once those requirements are met, compare the complete confirmed price. A material preference becomes much easier to apply when it refers to a figure you can actually house and maintain.

In the current AIB Robot catalog, silicone companions use suggested-retail references from 4 October 2025. STPE companions show a current price dated 15 November 2026. Those dates and price types belong in the comparison. Material choice also leaves the head pairing, warehouse configuration, accessories, and destination to be confirmed.

## Make a shortlist with four decisions

| Your main consideration | Compare first | Ask before choosing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Handling a full-size figure | Net weight, body dimensions, route into the room | What is the confirmed weight of this configuration? |
| A particular face or hair finish | Exact head reference, hair type, neck connection | Is this head supplied on the unit being offered? |
| A manageable care routine | Approved cleaning, drying, renewal, and clothing guidance | Which products should I use for this head and body? |
| A defined budget or time frame | Complete quotation and actual stock or production status | What is included, and which details are still being confirmed? |

Start with the [collection](/shop), choose a small number of bodies, and keep their product links together. The [materials overview](/blog/tpe-vs-stpe-vs-silicone) explains the terminology. This guide concentrates on turning that information into an order you can compare.

## First decide where the figure will live

Write down the place the figure will normally sit or stand. Include the support or chair, the space in front of it, and the room needed for dressing and cleaning. A photograph can hide the equipment and open space used to arrange the pose.

Measure the narrowest doorway and the tightest turn between delivery and storage. Check stairs, thresholds, and whether another person can help when needed. The unpacked figure and the shipping carton create different handling tasks, so request packaging dimensions if the route is restricted.

A tall body does not automatically weigh more than every shorter one. The current catalog lists a 175 cm silicone companion at 38 kg, while a 163 cm H-cup STPE companion is listed at 51 kg. The proportions and construction matter. These examples show why you should compare each figure's actual weight.

Keep privacy in the plan as well. If you want a cover or a private storage area, measure it before ordering. A convenient everyday position reduces unnecessary moving and makes care more manageable. The [height and weight guide](/blog/how-to-choose-height-cup-and-weight) provides a measurement worksheet.

## Compare the bodies at the same level of detail

A silicone body and an STPE body may be described differently. Some product names include a cup label. Others rely on height and a full measurement table. Write the measurements into the same columns so the naming style does not obscure the differences.

Full bust, underbust, waist, hips, shoulders, and feet can affect clothing and support. Use only the numbers on the correct product page. Where a number is missing, ask for it. A visually similar body from another page cannot supply a reliable substitute.

For example, the current 165 cm silicone body is listed at 32 kg, with an 81 cm full bust and 92 cm hips. The 166 cm C-cup STPE body is listed at 33 kg, with an 81 cm full bust and 84 cm hips. Their similar heights and bust measurements conceal a meaningful difference in the hip measurement.

That comparison becomes practical when you consider a chair, fitted trousers, or storage width. Copy the unit and measurement names exactly. A body code, a height, and a written head reference give the quotation a clear basis even when the photograph includes an older printed overlay.

## Decide which maintenance routine you will follow

Both materials need a clean environment, complete drying, careful handling, and suitable clothing. Their approved cleaners and finishing products may differ. Ask for care instructions for the complete ordered configuration, including a painted face, implanted hair, or electronic parts.

The elastomer guidance used on this site includes mild cleaning, thorough drying, and a light application of the recommended renewal powder when appropriate. Material-approved oil is used only where the care guidance calls for it. Plan somewhere clean to perform that routine without exposing a port, speaker, or wig attachment to powder or oil.

Silicone companions use care products intended for the finished silicone surface. Their painted areas and hair attachments also deserve attention. If your preferred routine involves minimal surface finishing, compare the actual supplied guidance for both candidates before assuming that a material name settles the question.

It helps to imagine an ordinary care session. Where will the figure rest? Can you reach all the relevant surfaces without an awkward lift? Where can it dry fully? The [elastomer care article](/blog/caring-for-tpe-and-stpe) and [silicone care article](/blog/silicone-doll-care-and-longevity) explain those tasks separately.

## Ask about feel and durability in useful terms

Describe what you want to know. You might prefer a surface that feels less tacky, a particular body shape, or a finish that works with your planned clothing. Those are questions a seller can address for a specific configuration or through a demonstration.

STPE notes supplied with this collection describe reduced oiling and greater durability compared with standard TPE. We have not laboratory-tested those comparative claims. Ask whether evidence refers to the material formulation, a sample test, or a completed figure and what kind of wear the claim concerns.

General polymer terminology also leaves room for variation. Avient explains that TPE formulations can be adapted for particular applications. That provides context for asking about the finished product's actual care and properties. [Avient's introduction to TPE](https://www.avient.com/resource-center/knowledge-base/article/beginner-s-guide-tpe)

A claimed number of years is less helpful than instructions for the daily conditions you can control. Discuss storage, clothing, cleaning, handling, and access to support. Keep a record of the condition on arrival so later questions can be assessed against that starting point.

## Confirm the face before treating the shortlist as final

The head in a supplied photograph may be an example pairing. Record the head you want, whether it is silicone or another material, its hair type, and the fitted neck connection. Ask whether the quoted unit already has that configuration or requires a separate order.

A silicone head on an elastomer body can be an available hybrid, but the exact pairing still needs confirmation. Skin tone, neck size, head weight, and connection hardware can affect the result. The [hybrid guide](/blog/hybrid-silicone-head-and-tpe-body) explains the information to request.

Hair is a useful example of a visible option that should be itemized. The silicone options sheet dated 4 October 2025 lists implanted hair at an additional $150 and implanted real hair at an additional $280. These are historical option references, subject to current availability and quotation. A wig shown in a photograph should also be confirmed as included.

If you prefer a face strongly, send that reference with both candidate bodies. You may find that one confirmed pairing resolves the decision. That is a sound reason to choose the body, provided it also meets your handling and care requirements.

## Compare complete prices using one request

As of the product references used here, silicone companions show $1,900 or $1,950 suggested retail from the October 2025 sheet. STPE companions show $2,199 alongside a previous list price of $2,599 dated 15 November 2026. A requested TPE build receives its own price after confirmation.

Request current quotations for the same destination, preferred head, hair option, and accessory requirements. Ask which items are included and which are charged separately. Transport and any destination-related charges need their own clear explanation.

Keep the price type and date visible in your notes. An older suggested-retail number and a current advertised figure do not by themselves establish a material cost comparison. The current written offer for a complete configuration is the figure relevant to your decision.

Also review the [returns process](/returns). A selection that is difficult to handle or different from your personal preference after a sound delivery creates a different situation from a defect. Resolve measurements, appearance, and configuration questions while you can still change the proposed order.

## Treat warehouse and made-to-order choices explicitly

A warehouse list is a starting point for a stock inquiry. Ask which body, head, material, hair, and options are fitted to the specific unit. Confirm the ship-from location and the current status before relying on a photograph or an earlier list.

A made-to-order configuration may offer different choices, with its own confirmation and production process. A desired head change should be checked with the same care as any other option. Availability of a body does not establish availability of every combination.

If timing matters, explain the date you have in mind and request a realistic response for each candidate. Compare the answers on the same basis, including configuration confirmation, production where applicable, transport, and inspection. The [warehouse versus made-to-order guide](/blog/in-stock-vs-made-to-order) explains the distinction.

Keep a second choice only if it genuinely meets your requirements. Switching to a body you cannot handle simply because it appears on a stock list creates an avoidable problem after delivery.

## Keep an electronic-head choice as a full configuration

The expression-head figure has its own listing and technical questions. It is listed at 164 cm with head #1068, and its supplied material descriptions require confirmation. Its weight should be obtained for that specific configuration.

If you want moving expressions, use the [AI robot page](/ai-robots/wm-164-f-1068) to begin a separate comparison of head functions, control, charging, network behavior, and included parts. A material decision for a conventional companion body cannot settle those electronic questions.

Likewise, the optional [voice box](/voice-box) is an audio add-on with its own confirmed compatibility. Include it in the inquiry only if you want that feature, and ask how it affects the complete order.

## A short request that moves the decision forward

Send the two product links, preferred head reference, destination country, and your normal seated or supported-standing arrangement. Add the weight and space limitations that matter, along with any essential hair or foot option.

Ask for the confirmed material, net weight, included head and accessories, stock or production status, care instructions, and complete quotation. Keep those answers beside the measurements. Choose the configuration that satisfies your requirements and whose routine you are prepared to maintain.
