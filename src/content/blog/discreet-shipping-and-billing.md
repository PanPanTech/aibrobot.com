Discreet shipping starts with a plain outer carton, a delivery plan that suits your home and accurate information on every required document. Ask for the packing method, dispatch location, complete cost and arrival process before paying. AIB Robot requests unmarked outer packaging and confirms the actual arrangement with an accepted order.

The current website is an inquiry storefront. A figure in the inquiry bag is awaiting a unit, price and shipping confirmation. This is the stage to raise concerns about shared entrances, delivery notifications, stairs or customs documents. Those details can affect whether the proposed shipment is practical for you.

## Confirm the outer carton and delivery information

Ask whether the selected warehouse or production route can use a plain outer carton without product photography or a product name. The packing method is confirmed for the actual order because different dispatch locations may use different packing processes. An assumption based on another buyer's photograph is less useful than the current warehouse's confirmation.

A carrier still needs handling and address information. Tracking descriptions, sender details, invoices and customs documents can appear in systems beyond the exterior of the carton. Ask which information will be visible at the relevant stage, including any delivery notifications sent to your phone or email. We cannot establish a universal description for every carrier's tracking page.

Discuss who can receive the parcel and where it will be left or handed over under the confirmed service. If you share a home, arrange an appropriate delivery window or recipient where the carrier offers that option. Any alternative delivery address must also be suitable for the product, the legal destination and the size of the shipment.

## Identify the dispatch route before comparing timing

The [US](/in-stock-us) and [EU](/in-stock-eu) warehouse pages reflect lists received in November 2026. We reconfirm the unit, fitted head, material and availability before payment. A catalog entry does not publish a live unit count or reserve a unit while you consider it.

For made-to-order figures, ask for the configuration-specific preparation and dispatch information. Distinguish production, readiness for collection, carrier handover, customs clearance and final delivery. A production estimate covers only part of that journey. Save the latest written timing confirmation with the order.

If the proposed unit becomes unavailable, review the new option and its route before accepting a substitution. A different head, body material or warehouse may change the cost and timing. The [warehouse guide](/blog/in-stock-vs-made-to-order) explains the confirmation questions to ask.

## Request a delivered-cost breakdown

The website's public figure prices are dated references. STPE companions are shown at $2,199 and the expression-head figure at $3,099 as of 15 November 2026. Silicone companions use suggested retail from a list dated 4 October 2025. None of these figures alone establishes the delivered cost of your order.

Ask the seller to identify the body and head, accessories, packing or freight charges where applicable, tax treatment and charges payable to a carrier or authority. Record whether each amount is fixed, estimated or awaiting a destination check. Include the currency and any payment-provider conversion or transfer charges you need to budget separately.

For a cross-border shipment, ask who prepares the declaration and who handles a clearance question. The Commission's online-import guidance explains that route, goods and tax treatment can affect charges. Keep the final quotation explicit about the amount collected by the seller and any amount payable elsewhere. [European Commission online-import guidance](https://taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu/customs/customs-procedures-import-and-export/importation/buying-goods-online-coming-non-european-union-country_en)

For example, compare two quotations using the same body, head, accessories and destination. One may include freight while the other leaves it open. Put those unanswered amounts in a separate column before comparing totals. A lower displayed product price cannot settle a comparison with different included services.

## Keep the paperwork accurate

Use a commercial description that reflects the actual goods and transaction. The invoice and packing list should let you connect the shipment with the accepted configuration. Ask for corrections through the responsible seller or carrier when a document contains an error.

AIB Robot declines false descriptions, understated values and routing intended to avoid a destination prohibition. Describing a companion figure as an unrelated product cannot resolve an admissibility problem. Check unresolved legal questions before accepting the transaction, using the actual adult-realistic product and electronic options. The [destination-law guide](/blog/adult-products-and-the-law) provides a practical process.

Keep the quotation, accepted order, invoice and carrier reference together. If a clearance request arrives, check that it identifies your shipment and ask which document or payment is required. Provide information through the carrier's verified process. Avoid sending identity documents or payment details in response to an unverified message simply because a parcel is expected.

## Understand the current request and payment process

Checkout currently creates an order request. The website takes no card payment on these pages. Requests may be stored in the browser and offered as an email draft, so check that your message has actually been sent through the agreed channel. A locally saved request by itself does not establish that the seller received it.

The [terms](/terms) require confirmation of the specific unit, final price and shipping arrangement. Payment is arranged only through the accepted process once an appropriate processor is approved. Do not email a card number. Retain the written acceptance and a receipt showing what was paid and to whom.

Ask about the expected payment statement description before paying if that matters to your privacy. A blog article cannot supply an unconfirmed merchant descriptor or promise what a bank will show. Refund handling, when applicable, also needs to follow the accepted process and relevant consumer rules.

If you change an option after a quote, request a revised total. Adding the voice box, choosing another material or changing the destination can affect the order. Confirm that the new configuration replaces the earlier version in the written acceptance so there is one clear record to use at delivery.

## Plan for a heavy, long carton

Obtain the gross packed weight and dimensions. Body weight on a specification table excludes packaging and can leave out accessories supplied in separate cartons. The catalog includes 45 kg and 51 kg bodies. Check the access route and arrange suitable assistance or moving equipment before the delivery date.

Ask what the confirmed service includes at the building entrance, lift or stairs. Keep a clean, dry unpacking area ready and identify a padded resting surface that can support the figure. A plain carton remains a large object that needs a practical route through the home.

Discuss any insured transport option and the evidence needed for a claim with the seller. Ask who opens the claim and what the quoted arrangement covers. The presence of insurance wording does not establish an automatic payout or a particular remedy. Keep the agreed terms with the shipment records.

If the body and an electronic accessory travel in separate cartons, request each tracking reference and a packing list showing their contents. Check the expected parcel count at delivery. An accessory arriving later should be tracked against that list so that a partial delivery can be identified accurately.

## Inspect in a useful sequence

Photograph the outer carton and label before discarding anything. Record crushing, split seams, punctures or moisture. Open carefully, then photograph displaced or damaged inner packing before moving the figure. Finally, document the product and any affected area, including a wider view showing its location.

Check the delivered head, body material and accessories against the written configuration. If you see a tear, damaged connection or missing item, report that specific issue promptly. Keep the packaging while it is assessed. The [returns page](/returns) explains the current process for transit damage and suspected defects.

Avoid repairing, returning or repeatedly testing a damaged area before receiving instructions. An electronic component should be assessed with the supplied approved setup method. Keep the head, ports and power equipment dry during body cleaning. The [first-week guide](/blog/first-week-unboxing-and-setup) covers the transition from inspection to care.

## Deal with delays using the agreed order record

A created shipping label, carrier collection and a completed delivery are separate events. Ask for the latest confirmed stage if the information stops progressing. Keep replies with the order reference and use the accepted dispatch information when asking for an update.

Where the US Mail, Internet, or Telephone Order Merchandise Rule applies, sellers need a reasonable basis for shipment representations and must address covered delays through the required consent or refund process. The exact circumstances matter, so retain the promised timing and notices. [FTC business guidance on online-order shipping](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/business-guide-ftcs-mail-internet-or-telephone-order-merchandise-rule)

For an EU transaction, assess withdrawal and defective-goods rights under the applicable rules. A hygiene exception has conditions, and mandatory consumer protections remain relevant. The [EU buying guide](/blog/buying-a-companion-figure-in-the-eu) links the official explanations. Contact the seller with a clear account of the problem and the outcome you seek, then retain the response and any agreed next step.
