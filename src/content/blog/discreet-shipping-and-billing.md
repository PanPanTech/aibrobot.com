Privacy and paperwork are different jobs, and people get into trouble when they ask one to do the other. A plain carton is a reasonable request. A lie on a customs form is not. This site will do the first and will refuse the second.

## The carton

We ask for unmarked outer packaging: no product photograph and no product name on the outside. We confirm the method when we confirm the order, because a warehouse and a made-to-order shipment are not always packed by the same hands. We cannot control the words a carrier prints on its own tracking page. We will not pretend the contents are invisible to anyone who opens the box. Discreet means the hallway does not have to see a catalogue cover. It does not mean the shipment is a secret from the law.

Inside, the figure should be supported so the neck and joints are not taking the whole weight against a corner. If you added a voice box or you ordered the expression head, those parts should be packed so a port is not crushed. If the carton arrives crushed, photograph the outside before you unpack, then the packing, then the figure. That sequence is what makes a damage conversation possible. It is described again on the returns page.

## The paperwork

We will not describe a companion figure as a mannequin, a toy, a dress form, or any other category that is not true, in order to clear customs. We will not use a grey route, an under-declared value, or a third-country hop. If a destination can only be reached that way, we do not ship there. If the product is prohibited where you live, the order stops. You are responsible for knowing that law. We are responsible for not helping you evade it.

Duties and taxes, where they apply, are for the recipient unless we have agreed otherwise in writing. Freight is not hiding inside the figure price. It is confirmed with the destination and the weight. A price of $2,199 or $3,099 on a product page is the figure, not the delivered cost.

## The bill

Checkout is an order request stored in your browser, with an email draft if you choose to send it. No card is charged on the page. Do not send a card number by email. When a processor that can take this category of payment is in place, payment happens after we have confirmed the unit and the final price in writing. Until that confirmation, you have a request, not a contract, and we have not reserved a unit.

The indicated total in the inquiry bag can include the voice box at $100 if you ticked it. It does not include surprises we have not named. If a configuration changes — a different material, a head we cannot supply, freight you had not asked us to estimate — the number changes in writing before you pay. That is slower than a one-click shop. It is also how you avoid paying for a figure that was never the one in the photograph.

Discreet shipping is a packing choice. Honest paperwork is a limit. Both are part of the order, and only one of them is negotiable.
